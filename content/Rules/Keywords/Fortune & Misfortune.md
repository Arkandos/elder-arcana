When rolling with Fortune, you roll twice and use the higher die. When rolling with Misfortune, you roll twice and use the lower die.

Sources of Fortune and Misfortune cancel each other out 1-1. This means that if you have Fortune from two different sources and Misfortune from one, you still roll with Fortune since you have more Fortune than Misfortune.
