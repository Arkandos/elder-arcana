Enchanted items require you to attune to them to access their potential. This is a process of several steps, as you must first identify and understand the item, attune to it, and spend experience to unlock its powers.
# Identifying
To be able to attune, you must first understand the item. This is done with the [[Weave Whispers]] ability, where each success gives you additional information about it. Each successful use gives you two pieces of information about the next Rank of the items attunement.
1. What effect you gain when attuned to this Rank
2. What act must you perform to be able to attune to this Rank.

# Attuning
Attuning to an item is done with the [[Attune Item]] ability, which allows you to attune to one item per Rank. While it is not strictly necessary to first identify an item before attuning to it, you most likely want to know what you are getting yourself into beforehand. 
# Spending experience
Once you have attuned to an item and performed the act necessary to advance to the next Rank, you can spend the required amount of experience (during downtime as normal) to advance it as if it was an ability. Each Rank unlocks new effects that you benefit from as long as you stay attuned to the item.

*Example: You find an enchanted spear, and decide to identify it. After successful identification, you learn that the first Rank of the item gives the wearer a +1 bonus to their Maneuver Tests. The act to unlock the first Rank is to bring the spear to the midst of a forest. After you have done that, you can attune to the item and spend the necessary experience to bring it to Rank 1. At that point, you benefit from the +1 to your Maneuver Tests.*
