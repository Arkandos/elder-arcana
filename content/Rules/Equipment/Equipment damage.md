Normally when you are damaged, your equipment stays intact. But sometimes your equipment may be the actual target of an effect that damages it.

When equipment takes damage, its effectiveness may be reduced. For every 5 full points of damage an item takes in a single instance, you must reduce one of its relevant attributes by 1. 
>For example, if your Armor takes 15 physical damage, you must reduce its Physical Armor attribute by 3 (but record the original bonus before applying penalties!)

The penalties last until the item is fully repaired. An item that has one score reduced to 0 is broken and cannot be used.

Only permanent attributes can be drained (thus nothing that comes from a Power), but the bonuses from [[Augment Armor]], [[Augment Weapon]], and [[Attuned Items]] count as permanent.
* Armor has its physical or magic armor reduced when taking the appropriate damage.
* Shields have their defense reduced in the same way
* Weapons can have their static attack or damage bonus reduced.
