These minor magical objects are easily reproduced, and most are available for sale in any town or above.

# General Items
## Scoundrel's Patch \[Control\] \[Illusion\]
Tier 1. Cost 50. Weight 0.
These small patches of cloth and easily sown into any type of clothing and allow the wearer to more favourably conceal objects. The patch is enhanced with illusory magics that dampen sound and other sensory sensations.

## Thriving Rope \[Create\] \[Nature\]
Tier 1. Cost 100. Weight 1.
This piece of rope is only one meter long, but grows when water is applied to it. Each day, it can grow up to 20m of rope. The additional length lasts for four hours before withering away.

## Cat's Eye Charm \[Transform\] \[Shapeshifting\] \[Instilled\]
Tier 1. Cost 400. Weight 1.
While instilled, your maximum Health is reduced by 2 and you gain the ability to see in the dark for up to 20 meters.

## Glassteel Essence Container, Meager \[Control\] \[Esoteric\] \[Alteration\]
Tier 1. Cost 500. Weight 10.
This heavy and cumbersome container made of glass-steel inlaid with protective materials on the inside is meant to transport a single unit of [[The Weave#Essences|essence]] safely. However, the essence must still be safely moved into or out of the container.


# Travel Items

## Water Rod \[Sense\] \[Water\]
Tier 1. Cost 100. Weight 2.
This dark metal rod is always cool to the touch, and when activated helps to find water sources. When exploring in search of water, the wielder of the rod gains a +4 bonus to all Tests to locate water.
## Watcher's Helm \[Enhance\] \[Mind\]
Tier 1. Cost 250. Weight 2.
A heavy and decorated helmet that enhances the wearer's capacity to keep alert over extended periods of time. When keeping watch, the wearer gains a +2 bonus to Awareness and Initiative (for the first round of an encounter).
## Breeze Bag \[Create\] \[Air\]
Tier 1. Cost 300. Weight 1.
This large bag is woven with traces of air essence, which continually keep it cool and aerated while at the same time maintaining an air tight seal when the lid is closed. It is used to transport perishables like food or body parts to slow down their decomposition.
## Journeyman Boots \[Enhance\] \[Body\]
Tier 1. Cost 750. Weight 1.
These comfortable scale boots are imbued with Body-enhancing magics. The wearer is able to travel for 2 additional hours per day without suffering [[Fatigue]].
## Elemental Tent \[Create\] \[Air\] \[Earth\] \[Fire\] \[Water\]
Tier 1. Cost 1200 silver. Weight 30.
A heavy and cumbersome tent with four elemental enchantments that makes it especially useful for travellers in hostile environments. Large enough for 6 individuals, it keeps a comfortable internal temperature in all but the most extreme conditions. The air inside rotates and keeps fresh, while the surface of the tent is stabilised. Resting for a full rest inside of the tent reduces [[Fatigue]] by 1 step.
