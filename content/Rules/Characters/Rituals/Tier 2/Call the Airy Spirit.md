# Call the Anima Spirit \[Ritual\] \[Control\] \[Thaumaturgy\]

| Prerequisites: A Thaumaturgy Art of 5                                 | Time: 3 Phases                                 |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Components: A prepared circle. A pure source of the relevant element. | Tests: Thaumaturgy; Craft, Lore (Magic/Nature) |
Effect: You choose one of the Thaumaturgic arts that you possess and attempt to summon an [[Magic Creatures#Anima|Anima]] of that type, making an INT+Rank Test. The difficulty of the Tests to summon it depends on several factors. The closest location it could naturally manifest, the strength of the spirit, and how long you intend to bind it for. It is not recommended to attempt to summon a spirit with a might beyond your Art Rank, for such an attempt may be dangerous if not lethal.

During the ritual, other ritual participants may assist the process with a variety of means. The most common ways to assist this ritual is with Craft (to make or assemble something appealing to the spirit), or Lore (Magic/Nature) to provide additional knowledge and aid.

Success summons the spirit (which may be instant, or take some time depending on the nearest source it could naturally manifest) for a period of time as decided upon when starting the ritual. Since Anima cannot communicate or be seen by normal kith, you must use abilities like [[Second Sight]] and [[Anima Speech]] (or other magical effects). You and the other participants may bargain, threaten, or entice the spirit to perform tasks for you. It is easier to convince an Anima to do something it is naturally inclined to do, and impossible to force them to do something antithetical to their nature (like telling a spirit of water to enter lava). The spirit may ask for resources, Weave or having tasks completed for it as payment.

Once the agreed upon tasks have been completed (or the duration elapsed) the spirit returns to where it came from. Most Anima have little direct combat use due to their incorporeal and aloof nature, but clever Kith can still use them to their advantage.

Spending Essence can greatly increase the duration of this Ritual, and even make bindings permanent.
