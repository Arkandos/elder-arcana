# Charm of the Foolish Warrior \[Ritual\] \[Control\] \[Body\]

| Prerequisites: Body 5 | Time: 1 Phase             |
| --------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Components: An effigy | Tests: Body (8); Medicine |
Effect: You enchant the body of a participant in the ritual so that they may ignore the penalties of Wounds and [[Fatigue]], binding the effect to an effigy. Make a WIL+Body (8) Test to create the effect. If the target is separated from the effigy, they lose the benefits of this ritual until they are in contact again.

Unless a successful DEX+[[Medicine]] Test of at least 1/4th the targets max Health is made by another Ritual participant, the target takes 5 Raw Damage.

The power of the ritual lasts for 1 hour after its completion. When it ends, the target gains 2 points of Fatigue. You cannot use this Ritual on targets with 6 or more points of Fatigue.
