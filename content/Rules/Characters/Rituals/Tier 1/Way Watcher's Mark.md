# Way Watcher's Mark \[Ritual\] \[Sense\] \[Farsight\]

| Prerequisites: Farsight 3           | Time: 3 Phases      |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Components: Crushed eyes, 3 Health. | Tests: Farsight (8) |
Effect: Choose an area or a gateway within reach. For 1 day, whenever a creature passes through the chosen place the GM makes a hidden Awareness Test for you using your Farsight Rank to detect them. If you succeed, you see the area for a short time.

Detecting the Way Watcher's Mark has a TN of 3 + twice your Farsight Rank.
