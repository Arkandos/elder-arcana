# Thought to Thought \[Ritual\] \[Create\] \[Mind\]

| Prerequisites: Mind 3                | Time: 1 Phase                            |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| Components: A clear crystal, 3 Weave | Tests: Mind (8), Social Tests (see text) |
Effect: You choose a creature that you can see and project a telepathic message into their mind. The message may contain up to one word per Rank. The recipient knows the words are of supernatural origin, and may recognise your voice. If you score an additional success on the Mind Test, the words are received in whatever language the creature best understands.

You or another ritual participant may enhance the emotion of the message by performing a Test using a social ability like [[Charm]] or [[Command]].
