# Chamber of Effortless Weaves \[Ritual\] \[Enhance\] \[Esoteric\]

| Prerequisites: Esoteric 3               | Time: 3 Phases      |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Components: A prepared circle, 6 Weave. | Tests: Esoteric (8) |
Effect: Until the circle is broken, the weave is easier to manipulate within the circle. All Esoteric Ritual Tests made within the circle gain a +4 bonus, and creatures inside it can [[Channel]] as a Minor action, but only regain half the normal amount of Weave when doing so.
