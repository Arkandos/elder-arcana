# Shape Rigidity \[Ritual\] \[Transmute\] \[Alteration\]

| Prerequisites: Alteration 3                     | Time: 1 Phase         |
| ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Components: A rigid or flexible item, 12 Weave. | Tests: Alteration (8) |
Effect: You trace a circle on the floor, with a diameter no more than 1m per Rank. Choose if you wish to turn all non-magical items within the circle either rigid or flexible. Then supply one such item. When the ritual is completed, the items remain in that state for Rank hours before returning to their natural state.
