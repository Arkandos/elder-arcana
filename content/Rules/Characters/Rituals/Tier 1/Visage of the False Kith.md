# Visage of the False Kith \[Ritual\] \[Create\] \[Illusion\]

| Prerequisites: Illusion 3               | Time: 1 Phase       |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Components: Figurine of a Kith, 6 Weave | Tests: Illusion (8) |
Effect: You create an audiovisual illusion of a Kith for Illusion Rank hours, during which time it obeys your verbal commands. The illusion is faulty and does not stand up to scrutiny, nor can it mimic specific individuals. It can make noises, but not speak.

If you score one additional success, the quality of the illusion is improved.
If you score two additional successes, the illusion can speak and visually mimic a specific individual.
