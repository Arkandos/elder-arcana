# The Climber's Vines \[Ritual\] \[Create\] \[Nature\]

| Prerequisites: Nature 3                   | Time: 1 Phase     |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Components: Fertile soil, a seed, 6 Weave | Tests: Nature (8) |
Effect: From the soil a 3m wide mass of vines sprout over the duration of the ritual, clinging to the nearest wall which they climb up. They may climb up to 10 meters per Rank and last for 1 hour per Rank. Once the ritual is complete, they are suitable for climbing.
