# Face of Another \[Ritual\] \[Control\] \[Illusion\]

| Prerequisites: Illusion 3                       | Time: 1 Phase       |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Components: A drawing of the disguise, 6 Weave. | Tests: Illusion (4) |
Effect: Choose a target that was present for the ritual. For Rank hours, their visual appearance changes to that of another kith of your design. Imitating a specific individual requires an additional success.

Anyone interacting with the target in a way that could disprove the illusion makes a Disbelieve (8+Rank) Test.
