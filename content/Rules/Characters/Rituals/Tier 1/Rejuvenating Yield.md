# Rejuvenating Yield \[Ritual\] \[Create\] \[Transmute\] \[Nature\]

| Prerequisites: Nature 3                 | Time: 6 Phases                |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Components: A fresh fruit or vegetable. | Tests: Nature (8), at the end |
Effect: A living creature is enveloped in plant growths at the start of the ritual, where it is held in a comfortable and relaxing position. If it breaks out before the end of the ritual, it suffers 2 points of Fatigue from the sudden shock. Staying in the plant counts as 4 hours of rest for the purpose of recovering from Fatigue.

At the end of the duration, make the ritual Test (instead of at the beginning). Success means the target may spend a Recovery, ignoring all Wound or disease penalties to the recovery total. If the target suffers from a toxin (or its aftereffects) compare the Test result + 8 to the TN to recover, possibly ending it.
