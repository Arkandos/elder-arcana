Rituals are the way to create magnificent or lasting magical effects. To cast a ritual you must first possess a written copy of it. Ritual writings are inscribed with expensive or rare materials..
When casting a ritual, you must fulfill all the prerequisites and costs of the ritual. This includes one or more Tests that must be performed. When they have all been fulfilled, the ritual takes effect. Due to the lengthy time required for a Ritual, [[Characters#Attributes|Mythos]] cannot be used nor can other [[Fortune & Misfortune|Fortune]] effects. 

Failing to use a ritual can have catastrophic effects, depending on the amount failed by. Failing by 5 or less causes serious strain to the bodies of the casters, dealing 1d6 Magic damage to them per point they failed by. Failing by more than 5 instead has the result of a botch, which if the ritual included Essence can have serious consequences.
