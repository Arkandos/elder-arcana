Symbionts are plant-humanoid hybrids born from relations between powerful magic spirits of nature and kith. Their plant nature means they have a natural relation to nature, and require both sunlight and plenty of water to function. Most symbionts are social outcasts, having spent the majority of their life far away from society and may struggle to adapt.
- Attribute changes:  
	- STA +1, WIL +1, max WIL increased by 1.
	- DEX -1, PRE -1, min PRE decreased by 1.
- Speed modifier: none.
- Size:	Medium
- Starting abilities:
	- Language (1): Plant speech (see below)
	- Lore (Natural) (1)
	- Survival (2)
- Special traits:
	- Plant speech: You can communicate with non-sentient plants. This is a slow and arduous process as plants do not speak quickly. Each Phase spent costs 1 Health. Plants have a good memory of everything that came into contact with them, and track time by external events.
	- Armor symbiosis: You have +3 Physical and +2 Magic Armor, but cannot wear normal armor. You can only wear Living armor.
	- Verdant life: You eat only half as much as other kith of your size, but consume three times as much water. Every day you must get at least two hours of sunlight or you start to wither. Every time you take a rest while withering, make a STA Test of 5 + the number of days without sunlight. Failure means you lose 5 maximum Health until you stop withering.
