Dwarves are long-lived, short, and sturdy. Nicknamed the children of the rock, most dwarven societies are underground. Dwarven craftsmen are renowned and a point of pride. Elves and dwarves are natural friends, both enjoying the beauty of craftsmanship.  
- Attribute changes:  +1 STA, +1 WIL, max  STA increased by 1. 
- Speed modifier: -3
- Size:	Medium
- Starting abilities:
	- Bargain (2)
	- Craft (1)
	- Language (1): Dwarven
- Special traits:
	- Stubbornness: Your Dexterity or your Equipment you wear cannot reduce your Initiative below 3, or Speed below 7. 
	- Hardy: +2 bonus to Magic Armor, and +4 to all tests to resist diseases & poison.
