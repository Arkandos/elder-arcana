# Ward against Projectiles \[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Warding\] \[Force\] \[Flow\]
Tier 1.
Major Action. 3 Weave.
Melee, (3 + INT)m radius Zone
Effect: Make an INT+[[Weaving]] (8) Test.  Success creates a Zone that weakens incoming projectiles until the end of your next turn. Ranged attacks into the circle suffer a -4 penalty to damage.

Focus Major: The Zone lasts for (5 +Rank) rounds, and all Ranged attacks into the Zone suffer a -4 penalty. You cannot focus on it again.
>Additional Successes:  Increase the duration by Rank rounds; increase the damage penalty by 2.

>Enhance Weave: Increase radius by 2m (2); increase duration by 1 minute (1)

[[Flow]]: You can pay the Weave cost over multiple turns by using Free actions on your turn. It doesn't take effect until all the Weave has been paid.
