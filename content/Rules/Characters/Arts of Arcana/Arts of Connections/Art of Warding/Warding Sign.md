# Warding Sign \[Power\] \[Warding\]
Tier 1.
Minor Action. 1 Weave.
Effect: Choose a non-natural Origin and make a WIL+Rank Test (6). Success grants you 2 Armor against creatures and powers of that origin for 5+Rank rounds.

> Additional Successes: Increase duration by 5 rounds.

> Enhance Weave: Increase armor by 1 (3); choose an additional Origin (1)
