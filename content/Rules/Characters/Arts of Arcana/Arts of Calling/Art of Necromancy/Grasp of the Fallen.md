# Grasp of the Fallen \[Power\] \[Control\] \[Necromancy\]
Minor Action. 2 Weave.
Medium range, 1 corpse/bodypart and one enemy.
Attack: WIL+Rank vs Physical Defense
Effect: Success means that the corpse moves and grabs onto the target enemy. This deals 1d6+WIL Physical damage and reduces their Speed to 0 for 1 round, or until they free themselves.
> Additional Successes: +2 damage; increase duration by 1 round. 

> Enhance Weave: additional target (1); increase range by 10m (1)
