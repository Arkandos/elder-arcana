The fundamental forces of death and unlife. The undead, their spirits, and the bodies they left behind. While necromancy can affect living tissue, it cannot control them. Botches might free spirits, cause them to hinder you, or reveal faulty information.

| Rank | Sample effects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| :--- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Make superficial changes to a corpse in Melee range, like changing its colour or healing scars.<br>Learn how long a corpse has been dead.<br>Sense the presence of nearby undead spirits.                                                                                                                                                       |
| 2    | Make simple movements with a dead body part within Close range.<br>Sense if a corpse has been resurrected or reanimated.<br>Animate a simple Small Undead (like a bodypart) within Close range while you focus.<br>                                                                                                                             |
| 3    | Animate a simple Medium Undead within Close range while you focus.<br>Ask how a corpse died, which it need not answer truthfully. Asking the corpse does not involve its spirit or soul.<br>Make complex movements with a dead body part within Close range.<br>Detect the number of direction of nearby undead spirits.                        |
| 4    | Communicate with undead spirits who normally cannot.<br>Find out what killed a corpse, or removed its limb. Answers are general like "sword" or "claw", and given by how the corpse experienced it.<br>Animate a simple Medium Undead within Close range to guard an area for a day.<br>Trap a harmless spirit, or ward against lesser spirits. |
| 5    | Animate a group of simple Undead that follow your simple verbal commands.<br>Seize control of harmless undead while you focus.<br>Turn a corpse into dust, or into a skeleton.<br>Sculpt dead bodies, like combining multiple into one.                                                                                                         |
| 6    | Animate a complex undead for a long time that can react to a long list of triggers intelligently.<br>Contact or summon a specific undead spirit for a short time.<br>Summon a disembodied spirit to obey your commands for a short time.<br>Discover what anchors a disembodied spirit to the world.                                            |
| 7    | Animate a large group of simple undead that follows a series of commands for a long time.<br>Summon the spirit of a corpse, if available.<br>Destroy a disembodied spirit.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 8    | Entrap a soul permanently, until the trap is undone.<br>Detect where the soul or spirit of a body currently resides, even on other planes.<br>Summon a disembodied spirit to obey your commands for a long time.<br>                                                                                                                            |
| 9    | Temporarily bring the dead back to life, if their spirit is available.<br>Animate a sapient undead, capable of independent thought.<br>Become aware of all spirits and undead within Far range at all times.                                                                                                                                    |
