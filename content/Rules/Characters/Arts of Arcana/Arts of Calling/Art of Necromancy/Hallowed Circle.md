# Hallowed Circle \[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Ward\] \[Necromancy\]
Major Action. 3 Weave.
Melee, (3 + INT)m radius Zone
Effect: You create a Zone against spirits and undead for Rank minutes. Whenever a spirit or undead tries to enter the Zone, make an INT+[[Weaving]] Test against their Magic Defense. Success means that they cannot enter this round, and take 2d6+WIL+Rank Magic damage.
Failure means that they become immune to the Zone.
> Additional Successes: Increase damage by 2.

> Enhance Weave: Increase radius by 2m (2); increase damage by 2 (1); increase duration by Rank minutes (1)
