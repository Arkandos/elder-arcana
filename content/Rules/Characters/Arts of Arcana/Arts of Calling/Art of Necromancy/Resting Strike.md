# Resting Strike \[Power\] \[Destroy\] \[Necromancy\] 
Tier 1.
Major Action. 2 Weave.
Effect: Make a Melee Strike. Success deals WIL+Rank additional damage, or if the target is undead its wound severity is increased by 1 step (bypassing any normal immunities they may have).
You may spend additional successes on the Melee strike on these effects:
>Additional Successes: Increase knockdown TN by 2; push the target 4m; force another target in Melee range to also take the additional damage or increase its wound severity (your choice)
