# Murderous Ghost \[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Control\] \[Necromancy\] \[Summon\] \[Flow\]
Major Action. 6 Weave.
Medium Range
Effect: Make an INT+[[Weaving]] (6) Test. Success summons an incorporeal ghost at the target point for Rank rounds. At the end of the round, it flies up to (10+INT)m towards the nearest living creature and attacks them (see below). It can be banished by dealing (5+Rank * 2) damage at once to its defenses of 10+Rank / 5+Rank. Every round it rolls Rank as its Initiative, moves and attacks as above.

Ghost Attack: INT+Weaving vs Physical Defense.
Success deals 4+1d6+1d4/WIL Physical damage, +1d6 per additional success.

> Additional Successes: Increase duration by 2 rounds;

> Increase range by 10m (1); increase damage by 2 (1); increase duration by 2 rounds (1)

[[Focus]] (Major): Make an INT+Weaving Test against the ghosts Magic Defense. Success means it cannot move this round, while an additional success means it is banished.

[[Flow]]: You can pay the Weave cost over multiple turns by using Free actions on your turn. It doesn't take effect until all the Weave has been paid.
