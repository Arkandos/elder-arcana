# Hunger from Beyond \[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Control\] \[Convoking\] \[Flow\]
Major Action. 6 Weave.
Medium range, 2m radius Zone
Effect: Make an INT+[[Weaving]] (8) Test.
Success makes the Zone last for Rank rounds. At the end of your turn, the Zone pulls all creatures in it 2m towards the center, and you make an INT+Weaving Attack Test against the Magic Defense of the target closest to the center. Success means the target takes 2d6+WIL Physical damage and is Staggered for 1 round.
> Additional Successes (initial Test): Increase duration by 2 rounds; increase the radius by 2m
> Enhance Weave: increase duration by 2 rounds (1); the Zone attacks an additional creature closest to the center (2)

[[Focus]] (Minor): Move the Zone up to Rank meters, pay 1 Weave to move it an additional WIL meters.

[[Flow]]: You can pay the Weave cost over multiple turns by using Free actions on your turn. It doesn't take effect until all the Weave has been paid.
