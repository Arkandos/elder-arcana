# Mockingimp \[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Create\] \[Convoking\]
Major Action. 4 Weave.
Medium range, 1 enemy
Effect: Make an INT+[[Weaving]] Test against the Social Defense of all targets.
Success means the target's Speed is reduced by WIL, and it is [[Battered]] for Rank rounds. At the end of their turn, they may Rally WIL (Rank + 5) to end the effect.
> Additional Successes: Increase duration by 2 rounds; increase the Speed penalty by Rank

> Enhance Weave: Increase range by 10m (1); additional target (2)
