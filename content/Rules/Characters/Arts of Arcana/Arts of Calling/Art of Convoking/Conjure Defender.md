# Conjure Defender \[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Create\] \[Convoking\]
Tier 1.
Major Action. 3 Weave.  
Effect: Make an INT+[[Weaving]] (6) Test. Success increases your frontal Physical and Magic defense by 2 for (2+Rank) rounds.
