# Dreamer's Embrace \[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Control\] \[Mind\]
Major Action. 6 Weave.
Close / Medium range, 1 enemy
Effect: Make an INT+[[Weaving]] Test against the Magic Defense of all targets.
Success deals means the target takes a -2 penalty to Tests and Speed for 1 round.

>Additional Successes: increase the penalties by 2; you gain a +2 bonus to the first Focus Test.
>Enhance Weave: increase both ranges by 10m (1); additional target (2)

[[Focus]] (Major): Repeat the above Test against the same targets. 
Success means the target is [[Stunned]] until the end of your next turn, while failure means they are [[Staggered]] instead. Another creature may spend their Major action to shake the target awake, removing the Stunned or Staggered condition.

Focus (Minor, 2 Weave): As Major, but repeat the Test only against Stunned or Staggered targets.
