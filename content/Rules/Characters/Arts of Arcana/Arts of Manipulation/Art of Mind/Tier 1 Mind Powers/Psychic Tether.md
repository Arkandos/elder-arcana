# Psychic Tether \[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Destroy\] \[Mind\]
Major Action. 2 Weave.
Medium range, 1 enemy
Effect: Make an INT+[[Weaving]] Test against the Magic Defense of the target.
Success deals 1d6+WIL Magic damage, and the target becomes Tethered to you until your next turn. If you use this Power again, all your previous Tethers end.
The target may as a Minor action Rally WIL (5+Rank) to end this effect.
> Additional Successes: Increase the damage by 2; increase the Rally TN by 2 this round; Tether another target within 2m of a Tethered target.

[[Focus]] (Major): Your Tethers last until your next turn. Make a new INT+Weaving Test against the Magic Defense of all Tethered targets. Each success increases the future damage rolls of this Tether by 1d6. Then if you got at least one success, the target takes 1d6+WIL Magic damage. 
Focus (Minor, 1 Weave): As Focus Major.
