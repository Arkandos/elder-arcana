# Emotional Fulcrum \[Power\] \[Control\] \[Mind\]
Tier 1.
Major Action. 1 Weave.
Effect: Choose an emotion and make a WIL+Rank Test (6). Success means your Mental Defense is 3 higher against that emotion, but 3 lower against its opposite for 1 Phase. You clearly exhibit the opposite emotion for the duration of the effect, and are more vulnerable to having it manipulated.

> Additional Successes: Increase duration by 2 minutes.

> Enhance Weave: Affect an ally in Melee range instead (2); Increase duration by 1 Phase (2)*
