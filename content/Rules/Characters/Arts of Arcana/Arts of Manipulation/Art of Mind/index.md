Subtly influence the minds of others. Botches to use Mind may affect yourself, believe your effect worked when it did not, or make them aware of your attempts.

| Rank | Sample effects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| :--- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1    | Detect the most prevalent emotion of a creature within Close range.<br>Conceal your own emotions while you focus.                                                                                                                                       |
| 2    | Speak telepathically to a creature within Close range.<br>Make someone within Close range recognise you as someone else while you focus.<br>                                                                                                            |
| 3    | Strengthen an existing emotion within Medium range, like increasing an angry person's anger.<br>Redirect someone’s emotion towards another creature in Close range while you focus.<br>Implant a harmless urge within Close range, like turning around. |
| 4    | Implant an urge within Medium range, like going around a corner.<br>Speak telepathically to a creature within Medium range, and allow them to respond.<br>Create a natural emotion within a creature in Medium range.                                   |
| 5    | Implant a harmless command into someone's mind which they will execute immediately.<br>Change someone's state of consciousness within Close range.<br>Change a creatures emotion to another, or redirect a group's emotion.                             |
| 6    | Reduce the mental capacity of a creature to that of a child for a moderate time.<br>Create an overwhelming amount of grief, dread, or fear in a creature in Medium range, enough to occupy all their time.<br>Calm a riot                               |
| 7    | Foster loyalty in a creature, like a dog to its master (without overruling any previous allegiances).<br>Implant a violent command into a group of creatures in Medium range, like inciting a riot.                                                     |
| 8    | Implant a murderous command into a singular creature, which it will pursue for a very long time.                                                                                                                                                        |
| 9    | Dominate a creature in Melee range for a very long time, forcing them to obey your every command.                                                                                                                                                       |
