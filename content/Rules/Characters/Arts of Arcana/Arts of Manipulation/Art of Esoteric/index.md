The Esoteric art is about manipulating the flow of the weave itself. It can affect and manipulate other arts. Botches can cause any manner of effects, twisting and affecting your uses of Weave.


Tier 1 Exploration Effects

| Rank | Sample effects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| :--- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1    | Taint an object with magic so that it detects as weave bearing for a long time.<br>Detect if a creature within Medium range is a weaver.<br>Detect magical auras within Medium range.                                                                                                                                                             |
| 2    | Determine the power of a weaver you can see.<br>Determine the power and origin of a magical aura.<br>Dispel a weak magical effect within Close range.<br>Enhance the range of a different art, adding this Rank to the range for the target art.<br>Examine essence, its type and amount.                                                         |
| 3    | See which objects have sympathetic connections.<br>Mask a weavers signature, or make a mundane creature detect as a weaver.<br>Safely transfer or move essence within Close range while you focus.<br>Enhance the area of a different art, adding this Rank to the area affected.<br>Enhance another art so that it targets an additional target. |
