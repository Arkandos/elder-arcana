The Esoteric art is about manipulating the flow of the weave itself. It can affect and manipulate other arts. Botches can cause any manner of effects, twisting and affecting your uses of Weave.

| Rank | Sample effects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| :--- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Detect if a creature within Medium range is a weaver.<br>Detect magical auras within Medium range.<br>Taint an object with magic so that it detects as weave bearing for a long time.                                                                                                                                                |
| 2    | Determine the power of a weaver you can see.<br>Determine the power and origin of a magical aura.<br>Dispel a weak magical effect within Close range.<br>Examine essence, its type and amount.                                                                                                                                       |
| 3    | Mask a weavers signature, or make a mundane creature detect as a weaver.<br>Safely transfer or move essence of any Art within Close range while you focus.<br>Enhance the range of a different art, adding this Rank to the range for the target art.                                                                                |
| 4    | See which objects have sympathetic connections.<br>Enhance another art so that it targets an additional target.<br>Enhance the area of a different art, adding this Rank to the area affected.                                                                                                                                       |
| 5    | Reverse the effect of another art as it is being cast, like turning water to fire.<br>Enhance the range of another art while it is being cast by 1 step.<br>Suppress a magical effect for a time depending on its strength.<br>Identify the source of a magical effect as a unique identifier.<br>Mask the traces of your own magic. |
| 6    | Allow one sense to detect magical auras, and the origin of creatures.<br>Mask an aura, so it is not detected for a long time.                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
