# Unseen Hand \[Power\] \[Control\] \[Force\]
Major Action. 1 Weave.
Close range
Effect: You exert force or manipulate an object within Close range as if used by Kith with +0 STR. If delicate movements are desired, you must make a DEX+Rank Test to succeed, the TN being 3 or more higher than doing it manually.
> Enhance Weave: increase range by 10m (2); change Focus to a Minor action (3)

Focus (Major): The effect persists.
