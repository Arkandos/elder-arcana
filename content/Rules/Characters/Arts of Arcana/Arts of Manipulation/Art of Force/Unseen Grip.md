# Unseen Grip \[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Control\] \[Force\]
Tier 1.
Major Action. 3 Weave.
20/40m, 1 enemy
Effect: Make an INT+[[Weaving]] Test against the Magic Defense of all targets.
Success means you can slide them (Rank+WIL) meters. For Rank rounds, the target must make a STR Test to move, opposed by your WIL Test.
> Additional Successes: Increase the slide distance by 2m.

> Enhance Weave: 1 addtional target (2); increase slide distance by 1m (1)

Focus (Major, 1 Weave): Repeat the INT+Weaving Test against the targets, sliding them (Rank+WIL) meters if you succeed.
