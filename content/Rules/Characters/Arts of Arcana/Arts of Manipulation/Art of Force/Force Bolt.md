# Force Bolt \[Power\] \[Create\] \[Force\] 
Tier 1.
Major Action. 1 Weave.
Effect: Make a Ranged Strike. It deals no damage but instead immobilizes the target for Rank rounds, or until it is attacked. At the end of their turn, they may Rally STR (5+Rank) to end the effect.
You may spend additional successes on strike on these effects:
>Additional Successes: 1d6 Magic damage; push the target Rank meters.
