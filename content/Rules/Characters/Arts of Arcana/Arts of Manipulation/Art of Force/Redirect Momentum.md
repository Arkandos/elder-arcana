# Redirect Momentum \[Power\] \[Control\] \[Force\] \[Evade\]
Tier 1.
Reaction. 1 Weave.
Trigger: An attack is made against you
Effect: Make a DEX+Rank Test against the triggering attack result.
Success means the attack misses you, and you can redirect it towards a different creature within Rank meters of you.
> Additional Successes: Increase the redirect range by Rank meters.

Evade: You can only use one Evade ability against a particular effect. Evade Tests only suffer Wound penalties (not other Test penalties)
