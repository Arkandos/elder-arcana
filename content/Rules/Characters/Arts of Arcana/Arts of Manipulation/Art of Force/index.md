Move things at range, and create fields of invisible force. Botches to use Force often result in the target being dropped, moved in the wrong direction or with too much force.

Tier 1 Exploration Effects

| Rank | Sample effects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| :--- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Slowly move an object no larger or heavier than a sword within Close range.<br>Throw a small object from somewhere within close range.<br>Hold a willing target floating within Melee range while you focus.<br>Create a personal field to keep out mild weather like rain or wind.                             |
| 2    | Slowly lift a willing target straight up or down, up to close range.<br>Quickly move an inanimate object no larger or heavier than a sword.<br>Utilise a skill at range, like picking a lock but with some difficulty.                                                                                          |
| 3    | Create a stationary forcefield within Melee range that can protect against mundane dangers like storms or falling rocks.<br>Quickly throw objects no larger or heavier than two-handed weapons within Medium range.<br>Block out all non-extreme weather.<br>Hold a willing target floating within Close range. |
