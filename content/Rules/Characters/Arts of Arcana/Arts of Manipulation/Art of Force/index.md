Move things at range, and create fields of invisible force. Botches to use Force often result in the target being dropped, moved in the wrong direction or with too much force.

| Rank | Sample effects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| :--- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Slowly move an object no larger or heavier than a sword within Close range.<br>Throw a small object from somewhere within close range.                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 2    | Create a personal field to keep out mild weather like rain or wind.<br>Hold a willing target floating within Melee range while you focus.                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 3    | Quickly move an inanimate object no larger or heavier than a sword.<br>Utilise a skill at range, like picking a lock but with some difficulty.                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 4    | Slowly lift a willing target straight up or down, up to close range.<br>Create a stationary forcefield within Melee range that can protect against mundane dangers like storms or falling rocks.<br>Quickly throw objects no larger or heavier than two-handed weapons within Medium range.                                             |
| 5    | Block out all non-extreme weather.<br>Suspend objects in the air to create a stairway or bridge.<br>Move a heavy object like a wooden beam within Close range or lighter object within Far.<br>Slowly lift a willing target in any direction up to medium range.<br>Create a strong stationary forcefield in a line within Close range. |
| 6    | Create a strong stationary forcefield within Close range in an unnatural shape.<br>Suspend all free items in a room in the air, or toss them around.<br>Quickly propel a willing target within Close range a short distance.<br>Hold yourself floating, or hold a group of creatures floating.                                          |
| 7    | Immobilize and lift a creature in Far range.<br>Lift a massive or very heavy object like a small house within Medium range.<br>Create an invisible forcefield that wards off lesser creatures and phenomena like water.                                                                                                                 |
| 8    | Quickly move a group of willing targets in any direction, including yourself.<br>Manipulate every door and window in sight range.                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| 9    | Crush a house in Far range.<br>Rip a gate out of a gatehouse, crush a wall section.<br>Turn a group weightless for a short time, or inflict crushing weight.                                                                                                                                                                            |
