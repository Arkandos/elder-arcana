# Trickster Warrior's Transformation \[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Control\] \[Illusion\]
Major Action. 1 Weave.
Melee range, you or 1 ally
Effect: Make an INT+[[Weaving]] (6) Test. Success means the target gains a +2 bonus to Melee attack and Evade Tests, and weapon damage rolls for Rank rounds. 
[[Disbelieve]] TN: 10+Rank. All Tests against the target count as Disbelieve Tests. 
>Additional Successes: increase the duration by 2 rounds; increase the Disbelieve TN by 2; affect Rank additional targets
> Enhance Weave: increase range to Close (1); increase duration by 2 (2)
