Illusions fool the senses, although fooling several senses at once is more difficult. Botches may create the wrong image, give you away, or cause you to be the only one that experiences them.

| 3    | A simple moving illusion affecting one sense like a patrolling guard within Close range.<br>Loud unintelligible noise within Medium range.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 4    | Intelligible speech within Medium range as long as you focus.<br>Visually disguise a willing creature within range as long as you focus.<br>Small simple moving illusion within Medium range.                                                                                                                                                   |
