# Resist Element \[Power\] \[Create\] \[Thaumaturgy\]
Major Action. 2 Weave.
Melee, you or 1 ally.
Effect: Choose one of your Thaumaturgy Arts, and whether you wish to protect against it or its opposite. Make a WIL+Rank Test (6) using that Art. Success means the target gains a +4 bonus to all Armor against the chosen element for 10 rounds (1 minute).

| Art    | Opposite |
| ------ | -------- |
| Air    | Earth    |
| Earth  | Air      |
| Fire   | Water    |
| Nature | Fire     |
| Water  | Fire     |

> Enhance Weave: Rank additional targets (2); increase Armor by 1 (2)

Instead of \[Thaumaturgy\], this gains the keyword of the Art used.
