# Poison Spray \[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Create\] \[Nature\]
Major Action. 3 Weave.
(5 \+ INT)m long 45° Cone
Effect: Make an INT+[[Weaving]] Test against the Magic Defense of all targets.
Success means the target is affected by a Toxin for Rank rounds, taking 1d6+Rank Raw damage every round. The target may Rally STA (3+Rank) to end the effect.
>Additional Successes: Increase damage on that target by 1; increase Rally TN by 2
>Enhance Weave: Increase the cone length by 2m (2); Increase cone angle by 45° (2); increase duration by 1 round (1);
