# Nature’s Sting \[Power\] \[Create\] \[Nature\]  
Major Action. 2 Weave.  
Effect: You create a Nature’s Sting Toxin for 1 Phase. It can be applied to any Weapon or projectile. A creature hit by an affected weapon is also hit by the Toxin if the attack deals damage (the toxin is then removed from the Weapon)  
When the toxin affects a creature, they must make a STA Test of (WIL+Rank) or become  poisoned, losing 1d6 Health every round for Rank rounds.
