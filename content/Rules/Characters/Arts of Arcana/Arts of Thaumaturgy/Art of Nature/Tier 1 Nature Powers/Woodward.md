# Woodward \[Power\] \[Ward\] \[Nature\] 
Major Action. 3 Weave.  
Effect: Make a WIL+Rank (6) Test. Success means you gain 5 [[Barrier]] and 1 Physical Armor for 10 rounds.  
>Additional Successes: Increase the [[Barrier]] by 2; increase the Physical Armor by 1; increase the duration by 10 rounds (1 minute).  

>Enhance Weave: Increase the duration by 1 Phase (2); apply the effect to an ally within Melee range instead (1)
