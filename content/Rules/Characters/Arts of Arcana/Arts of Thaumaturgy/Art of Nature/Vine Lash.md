# Vine Lash \[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Create\] \[Nature\]
Major Action. 2 Weave.
A point within Medium range.
Effect: Make an INT+[[Weaving]] (6) Test. Success creates a 1m plant at the target point for Rank rounds, +2 rounds per additional success.
Then make the following attack against a target within (10 + INT)m:
Melee Attack: INT+Weaving vs Physical Defense
Success deals 2d6+WIL Physical damage.
>Additional Successes: Increase damage by 2.

>Enhance Weave: Increase initial range by Rank meters (1); increase attack range by 2m (1); each attack targets an additional creature (3)

[[Focus]] (Major): Make the above attack again
Focus (Minor): Spend 1 Weave to make the above attack again.
