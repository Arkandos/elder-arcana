# Conjure the Ferocious Beast \[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Create\] \[Nature\] \[Summon\] \[Flow\]
Tier 2.
Major Action. 8 Weave.
Medium Range
Effect: Make an INT+[[Weaving]] (10) Test. Success summons a Medium Beast at the target point for Rank rounds. It always acts at the end of the round, where it moves and attacks the closest enemy.
It has the following statistics:

| Health: 20 + Rank         | Speed: 15               | Physical Defense: 12 + Rank | Magic & Mental Defense: 10 + Rank | Armor: 5 |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------------- | -------- |
| Melee Attack: 6 + Weaving | Melee damage: 2d6 + WIL | Rally & Knockdown:<br>WIL   |                                   |          |
>Additional Successes: increase duration by 2 rounds; increase range to Long 
>
>Enhance Weave: increase range by 10m (1); increase duration by Rank rounds (2); increase Armor or damage by 2 (2); summon a Large or Small Beast instead (2); you may Focus as a free action on this power (2)

Focus (Minor): You choose what the Beast does this round.

[[Flow]]: You can pay the Weave cost over multiple turns by using Free actions on your turn. It doesn't take effect until all the Weave has been paid.
