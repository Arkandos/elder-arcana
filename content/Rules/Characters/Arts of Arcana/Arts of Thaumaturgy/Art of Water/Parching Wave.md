# Parching Wave \[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Destroy\] \[Water\]
Tier 1.
Major Action. 3 Weave.  
(5 \+ INT)m long 45° Cone  
Effect: Make an INT+[[Weaving]] Test against the Magic Defense of all targets.  
Success deals WIL+Rank Magic damage, and gives them a \-2 penalty to Tests and Physical Defense for 1 round.  
>Additional Successes: Increase damage by 2 against that target; increase the penalties by 1. 

>Enhance Weave: Increase the cone length by 2m (2); Increase cone angle by 45° (1); increase the penalties by 1 (3)
