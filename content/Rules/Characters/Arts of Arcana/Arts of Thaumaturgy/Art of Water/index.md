The elemental forces of water and cold. Water refers to all three states: fluid, solid, and steam. Botches usually result in the wrong liquid, losing control, or creating too much liquid.

Tier 1 Exploration Effects

| Rank | Sample effects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| :--- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Create or destroy a small amount of water within Melee range. <br>Cosmetically change liquid within close range, like changing its colour or taste. Uncomfortably chill a small object.                                                                                        |
| 2    | Create enough water to fill a barrel.<br>Control a moderate amount of water while you focus.<br>Change the state of a moderate amount of water within Close range.                                                                                                             |
| 3    | Detect contamination of liquid in Melee range.<br>Move a small amount of water within Medium range, and change its state.<br>Change water to be slightly unnatural, like cold steam or warm ice.<br>Create a harmless natural liquid while you focus, like juice or olive oil. |
