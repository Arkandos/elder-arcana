The elemental forces of water and cold. Water refers to all three states: fluid, solid, and steam. Botches usually result in the wrong liquid, losing control, or creating too much liquid.

| Rank | Sample effects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| :--- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1    | Create or destroy a small amount of water within Melee range.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 2    | Cosmetically change liquid within close range, like changing its colour or taste.<br>Uncomfortably chill a small object.<br>Detect contamination of liquid in Melee range.                                                                                                                  |
| 3    | Create enough water to fill a barrel.<br>Control a moderate amount of water while you focus.<br>Change the state of a moderate amount of water within Close range, like turning ice into water or steam.                                                                                    |
| 4    | Allow one sense to detect contamination of liquid.<br>Change water to be slightly unnatural, like cold steam or warm ice.<br>Create a harmless natural liquid while you focus, like juice or olive oil.<br>Grant a group the capability to effortlessly swim for a long time.               |
| 5    | Create water in an unnatural shape within Medium range, like a wall of steam or a pillar of water.<br>Create a large amount of water in Close range.<br>Allow your senses to pierce water, steam, and ice within Far range.<br>Travel at moderate speed in water.                           |
| 6    | Speak to water in Close range while you focus. Water has poor memory but knows all it is in contact with.<br>Create a spring that lasts for a long time.<br>Freeze a creature solid in Close range.<br>Change a large amount of water, changing its taste or making ice that does not melt. |
| 7    | Severely chill a large area for a long time.<br>Make a significant change to water for a long time like allowing anyone to breathe in it, or make it deal continuous damage.<br>Freeze a large amount of water for a long time.                                                             |
| 8    | Travel at very high speeds through water.<br>Commune with water, and see all that it reflects.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 9    | Create a massive amount of water, like a large wave.<br>Create a large amount of water in a very unnatural shape like a massive column.<br>Freeze a lake solid.                                                                                                                             |
