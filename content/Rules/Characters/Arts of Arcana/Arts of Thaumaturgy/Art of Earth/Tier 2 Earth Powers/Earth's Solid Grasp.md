# Earth's Solid Grasp \[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Control\] \[Earth\]
Tier 2.
Major Action. 4 Weave.
Close / Medium range, 1 enemy
Effect: Make an INT+[[Weaving]] Test against the Magic Defense of all targets.
Success means they are [[Immobilized]] until they escape as earthen hands restrain them for Rank rounds.
They may escape by a Major action Rally STR (5+Rank) Test, or by anyone dealing (10+Rank) damage to the earthen hands, which reduce all damage taken by your Rank.
>Additional Successes: the target cannot break out on their own until your next turn; the target takes a -2 penalty to all defenses while Immobilized.
>
