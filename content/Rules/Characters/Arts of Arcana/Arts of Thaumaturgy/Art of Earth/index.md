The elemental forces of earth, metal, and acid. Botches may result in losing control of the target, transforming into the wrong type of earth, or affecting yourself. 

| Rank | Sample effects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| :--- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Slowly move a small amount of earthen or metal material within Close range while you focus.<br>Create a thin layer of dirt or sand over a surface for a short time.                                                                                                                                |
| 2    | Detect structural instabilities within view.<br>Cause a rock to glow brightly while you focus.<br>Transform one type of loose earth to another, like dirt to mud for a long time.                                                                                                                  |
| 3    | Create a moderate acid within Melee range while you focus.<br>Create a moderate pile of loose earth or rock within Close range for a moderate time.<br>Slowly move a large amount of earthen material.                                                                                             |
| 4    | Quickly move a moderate amount of earth-based material within Medium range. Sense vibrations in the earth, enough to detect the presence of creatures within Medium range.                                                                                                                         |
| 5    | Speak to earth while you focus. Earth has excellent memory but difficulty perceiving the movements of creatures.<br>Make your hearing unhindered by earth softer than metal.<br>Create and sustain loose earth or stone in an unnatural shape like a wall within Medium range for a moderate time. |
| 6    | Shape metal like it was clay in Melee range.<br>Create a simple stone structure, like a house or bridge for a long time.<br>Destroy a stone wall in Melee range, causing it to crumble.<br>Lift very heavy objects in Medium range like a portcullis.                                              |
