# Zephyrs Roar \[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Create\] \[Air\]
Major Action. 3 Weave.
Line 3m wide and Close long, all creatures.
Effect: Make an INT+[[Weaving]] Test against the Physical Defense of all targets. Success means they are pushed (5+WIL)m away from the origin.

The Line becomes a Zone until the end of the round. It is difficult terrain to move towards its origin, and advantageous terrain to move away from it (move twice as far as normal). Attacks in or through the Zone suffer a -4 penalty.

> Additional Successes: Increase push distance by 4m; deal 2d6 Physical Damage to a target pushed into a solid object
> Enhance Weave: increase Line width by 2m (2); increase Zone duration by 1 round (2); target pushed into solid object must make Knockdown Test against half your Test result (2); increase push distance by Rank (2);
