# Windcage \[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Create\] \[Air\]
Tier 1.
Major Action. 3 Weave.
Medium range, 1 enemy
Effect: Make an INT+[[Weaving]] Test against the Magic Defense of all targets.
Success means the target is moved 1d4+WIL m in a random direction, and immobilized for Rank rounds. They may Rally STR (5+Rank) to end this effect. At the start of your turn, the target is moved randomly again.
> Additional Successes: Increase duration by 2 rounds.

> Enhance Weave: Increase range by 10m (1); additional target (3)
