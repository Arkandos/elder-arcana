# Circling Winds \[Power\] \[Create\] \[Air\]
Major Action. 2 Weave.
Melee, you or 1 ally
Effect: Make a WIL+Rank (6) Test. Success grants a +2 bonus to Physical Defense, and you gain a 1m Aura for (1+Rank) rounds. At the end of every round, make a WIL+Rank Test against the Physical Defense of all creatures in the aura. Success pushes them 1m away from you.

> Additional Successes (initial test): Increase duration by 2 rounds; increase Aura radius by 1m.

> Enhance Weave: Increase defense bonus by 1 (1); increase Aura push distance by 2m (1)
