The elemental forces of weather, wind, and air. Botches may result in the wrong type of weather, drawing unwanted attention, or losing control of the effect.

| Rank | Sample effects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| :--- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Predict natural weather for the day.<br>Create or stop breezes within Medium range.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 2    | Control bursts of wind within Close range.<br>Create a mist that stretches out to Medium range while you focus.                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| 3    | Control gentle weather like rain within Medium range.<br>Cause an unnatural wind to circle a creature within Far range while you focus.<br>Be heard clearly regardless of noise.<br>Change a cosmetic property of air for a long time, like its colour or sound.                                                      |
| 5    | Control all winds within Close range.<br>Create a thunderclap or spark within Medium range.<br>Carry your voice to a place within Far range.<br>Create unnatural gentle weather, like rain indoors.<br>Shape a cloud to display a message or icon.                                                                    |
| 6    | Speak to air within close range while you focus. Air has fleeting memory of past events.<br>Create gentle weather in an unnatural shape within Medium range, like a cloud wall.<br>Create harsh weather like a thunderstorm.<br>Listen to a conversation within Far range.<br>Transform one type of air into another. |
| 7    | Control harsh weather within Far range.<br>Ward a creature or location against all mundane weather.<br>Fetch items within Medium range.<br>Summon and control lightning within sight range while you focus.                                                                                                           |
| 8    | Create dangerous air like a toxic or corrosive cloud within Far range, which severely harms or kills lesser creatures.<br>Fly at high speeds while you focus.                                                                                                                                                         |
| 9    | Control all natural weather you are aware of.<br>Create very unnatural weather, like turning rain into stones.                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
