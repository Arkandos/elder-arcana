# Scorching Metal \[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Create\] \[Fire\]
Major Action. 3 Weave.
Close range, 1 enemy wearing or holding metal
Effect: Make an INT+[[Weaving]] Test against the Magic Defense the target.
Success deals 2d6+WIL Magic damage. 
If the target is wearing or holding something primarily made of metal choose one of those items. The target takes a -2 penalty for 1 round to Defense if an armor or shield, or an equal penalty to attacks if a weapon. It also takes half the amount of damage again at the start of your next turn.
> Additional Successes: Increase damage by 2; if applicable the target suffers penalties to both Defense and attacks.

> Enhance Weave: increase damage by 2 (2); increase range by 10m (1)
