# Sword of Flame \[Power\] \[Create\] \[Fire\] \[Bound\]
Major Action. 2 Weave
Effect: Make a WIL+Rank (6) Test. Success creates the following Weapon you can use for 1 minute. When attacking with it, you may use WIL+Rank in place of DEX+Weapons (Melee).

At the start of your turn, you must pay 1 Weave. If you cannot, you suffer 1d6+Rank Magic damage, and increase the damage you suffer from this Weapon by 1d6.

> Additional Successes: Increase duration by 1 minute; increase Weapon damage by 2; increase its Range by 1m (once)
>Enhance Weave: Increase damage by WIL, but takes up an extra hand (1, once); targets that takes damage from the Weapon have their Magic Armor reduced by 2 for 1 round (2 ★)

Melee. 1 hand. 4+1d6+WIL Magic Damage.
