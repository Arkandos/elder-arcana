The elemental forces of fire, heat, and light. Supernatural fires that are not created by igniting existing materials do not spread. Botches usually result in an unintended target or intensity. 

| Rank | Sample effects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| :--- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1    | Ignite several easily combustible materials within Close range, like dry wood.<br>Cause an existing small fire like a torch to flash and spark within Close range.                                                                                                                                      |
| 2    | Create a simple light or fire within Melee range that does not harm you while you focus.<br>Ignite combustible material like wood within Close range.                                                                                                                                                   |
| 3    | Create a medium fire that burns without fuel or smoke for a long time within Close range.<br>Change the color of fire or light for a long time.                                                                                                                                                         |
| 4    | Create a harmless fire in Melee range while you focus.<br>Create bright moveable light within Medium range while you focus.<br>Extinguish medium fires within Medium range.<br>Move a small fire as you desire within Close range.<br>Chill a creature in Medium range to the bone, inflicting fatigue. |
| 5    | Create fire in an unnatural shape within Medium range, like a wall or a bird.<br>Extinguish small magical fires or large mundane ones.<br>Ward a creature or location against mundane fire.<br>Create a complex lightshow within Far range.                                                             |
| 6    | Speak to fire within close range while you focus. Fire has a good memory of things it has burnt or that has basked in its light.<br>Remove the shadow of a creature.                                                                                                                                    |
| 7    | Create fire in Far range that spreads.<br>Speak to an extinguished fire while you focus.<br>Create an unnatural fire, like one that burns underwater or without air.                                                                                                                                    |
| 8    | Create a simple animate fire, like a bird that follows your commands.<br>Extinguish all mundane fires in a neighbourhood.<br>Control all mundane fire you are aware of.                                                                                                                                 |
| 9    | Create a very unnatural fire, like one that burns inflammable materials like stone.<br>Ignite flammable material in Sight range.<br>                                                                                                                                                                    |
