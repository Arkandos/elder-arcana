# Circle of Flames \[Power\] \[Create\] \[Fire\]
Tier 1.
Major Action. 3 Weave.
2m burst Zone centered on you, all other creatures
Effect: You create a burning Zone for (5+Rank) rounds. Make a WIL+Rank Test against the Magic Defense of all other creatures in the Zone. Success means they suffer 4+Rank Magic damage.
At the start of your turn, repeat the Test to see if creatures suffer the damage again.
>Enhance Weave: Increase burst by 2m (1); make Rank creatures immune to the Zone (2); increase duration by Rank minutes (2)
