# Cursed Jig \[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Control\] \[Body\]
Major Action. 3 Weave.
(5 \+ INT)m long 45° Cone
Effect: Make an INT+[[Weaving]] Test against the Magic Defense of all targets.
Success means you may slide the target Rank m, and their Physical Defense is reduced by 2 for 1 round.
Additional Successes: Increase slide by WIL m;
Enhance Weave: Increase the cone length by 2m (2); Increase cone angle by 45° (2); increase duration by 1 round (2);
