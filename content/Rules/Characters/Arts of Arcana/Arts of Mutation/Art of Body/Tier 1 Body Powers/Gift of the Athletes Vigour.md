# Gift of the Athletes Vigour \[Power\] \[Enhance\] \[Body\]
Major Action. 1 Weave.
Melee, 1 target
Effect: Make a WIL+Rank Test (8). Success means the target gains a +4 bonus to [[Acrobatics]] and [[Traverse]] Tests for (5+Rank) rounds. 
>Additional Successes: increase bonus by +1; increase duration by Rank rounds.
>Enhance Weave: Rank additional targets (2); increase range to Close (1); increase duration to minutes instead of rounds (2)
