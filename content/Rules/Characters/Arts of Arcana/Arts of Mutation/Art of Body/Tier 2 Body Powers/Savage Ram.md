# Savage Ram \[Power\] \[Control\] \[Body\]
Major Action. 2 Weave.
Melee, 1 enemy
Effect: Make a STR+Rank Test against the Physical Defense of all targets. Success means they are pushed 5+Rank meters in a straight line.
You may follow along one target, shifting an equal distance after it.
> Additional Successes: The target must make a Knockdown Test, against a TN equal to (Rank + the number of meters it could not be pushed)
> Enhance Weave: additional target (2); increase range by 1m (1)
