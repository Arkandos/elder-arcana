Manipulate inanimate material, usually incapable of transforming magical material. Botches may result in an unwanted transformation, problematic fault in the creation, or receiving faulty information.

| Rank | Sample effects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |     |
| :--- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --- |
| 1    | Sense the composition of an object you touch.<br>Determine temperature within Close range.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |     |
| 2    | Write upon hard material like stone. Flavour, heat, or chill a small amount of matter while you focus.<br>Halve or double the weight of a small object while you focus.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |     |
| 3    | Harden or weaken a small amount of material for a long time.<br>Turn an object reflective, or a reflective object into a one-way mirror for a long time.<br>Transform a small amount of matter to a similar form, like copper to tin or water to ale for a long time.                                                                                                                                  |     |
| 4    | Transform a moderate amount of matter to a similar form for a long time.<br>Ruin mundane equipment within Medium range.<br>Repair simple construction like a door.<br>Create a hole within melee range large enough to peek through while you focus.<br>Slightly shrink or enlarge an object.                                                                                                          |     |
| 5    | Transform a moderate amount of matter within close range to a manufactured form, like turning steel into a sword or a branch to a plank.<br>Make a moderate change to matter in Close range, like making dirt shape able like clay or water solid.                                                                                                                                                     |     |
| 6    | Repair a moderately complex construction like a cart or a gate.<br>Significantly harden material for a long time, like making dirt as hard as stone.<br>Permanently change all cosmetic details of a moderate amount of matter, like its sound, colour, and taste.<br>Create a hole large enough to crawl through while you focus.<br>Significantly shrink or enlarge an object, or adjust its weight. |     |
| 7    | Significantly change matter for a short time, like turning stone bouncy or wood pliable.<br>Change an elements properties to another, like water that burns, or water that flows downhill.<br>Mold material into a simplistic mobile creature.                                                                                                                                                         |     |
| 8    | Completely change a fundamental property of an object for a moderate time, like removing its weight.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |     |
| 9    | Transform a creature in Melee range into a different material, or material into a mundane creature (like a log into a pig)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |     |
