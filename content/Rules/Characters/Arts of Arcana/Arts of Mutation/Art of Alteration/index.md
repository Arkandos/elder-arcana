Manipulate inanimate material, usually incapable of transforming magical material. Botches may result in an unwanted transformation, problematic fault in the creation, or receiving faulty information.

Tier 1 Exploration Effects

| Rank | Sample effects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| :--- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Sense the composition of an object you touch.<br>Write upon hard material like stone. Flavour, heat, or chill a small amount of matter while you focus.<br>Halve or double the weight of a small object while you focus.                                           |
| 2    | Harden or weaken a small amount of material for a long time.<br>Turn an object reflective, or a reflective object into a one-way mirror for a long time. Transform a small amount of matter to a similar form, like copper to tin or water to ale for a long time. |
| 3    | Transform a moderate amount of matter to a similar form.<br>Ruin mundane equipment within Medium range.<br>Repair simple construction like a door.                                                                                                                 |
