# Lubricious Cleave \[Power\] \[Create\] \[Alteration\] 
Tier 1.
Major Action. 3 Weave.
Effect: Make Strike. 
Then place a 2m long 180° degree cone that includes the target. If you made a Ranged Strike, the cone must be centered on the target and source its origin in your direction. If you made a Melee Strike, the cone must start adjacent to you.

Make a WIL+Rank Test against the Magic Defense of all targets in the cone.
Success: The target drops an item it was holding (determined randomly if multiple), but not attached items.
>Additional Successes: to pick up the dropped item before the start of your next turn requires a Major action.
