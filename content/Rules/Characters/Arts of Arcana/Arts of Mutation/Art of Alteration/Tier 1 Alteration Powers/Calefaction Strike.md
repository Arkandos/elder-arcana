# Calefaction Strike \[Power\] \[Transmute\] \[Alteration\] 
Tier 1.
Major Action. 2 Weave.
Effect: Make a Strike and choose either to heat or chill the weapon (or projectile for a ranged weapon). If the Strike hits, resolve the relevant effect below: 
* Heat: The target takes 2 additional damage, +1 / additional Success of the Strike.
* Chill: The target must make a STA (5+Rank) Test or gain 1 point of [[Fatigue]].

You may spend additional successes on the Strike on these effects:
>Additional Successes: Chill: force another target within 1m to also suffer the Chill effect*
