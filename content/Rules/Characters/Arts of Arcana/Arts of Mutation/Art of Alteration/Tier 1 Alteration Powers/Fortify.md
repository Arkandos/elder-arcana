# Fortify \[Power\] \[Enhance\] \[Alteration\]
Tier 1.
Major Action. 3 Weave.
Melee, 1 piece of armor
Effect: Make a WIL+Rank Test (6). Success increases the targets either Physical or Magic Armor by 3 for (5+Rank) rounds.

> Enhance Weave: Increase the duration by 1 Phase (2); the target gets half the bonus to the Armor type not chosen (1)
