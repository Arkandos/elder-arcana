# Sunderpoints \[Power\] \[Transmute\] \[Alteration\]
Major Action. 3 Weave.
Melee, Rank ranged projectiles
Effect: Make a WIL+Rank Test (6). Success enhances the projectiles so that they deal 5 additional damage, and reduces Physical Armor by 2 for 1 round. The enhancements last for (5+Rank) rounds.
> Additional Successes: Increase enhancement duration by 2 rounds; enhance Rank additional projectiles

> Enhance Weave: Increase damage bonus by 2 (1); enhance Rank additional projectiles (1); increase Armor penalty by 1 (2)
