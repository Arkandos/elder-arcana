# Terrain Spear \[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Transmute\] \[Alteration\]
Major Action. 2 Weave.
Close range, 1 enemy within 0,5m of a hard surface (like stone)
Attack: INT+[[Weaving]] vs Physical Defense
Success deals 2d6+WIL Physical damage.
> Additional Successes: Increase damage by 2; immobilize the target for 1 round or until it spends a Major action to get free.

> Enhance Weave: additional target (2); increase range by 10m (1); increase range from hard surface by 0,5m (1)
