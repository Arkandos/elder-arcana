# Burden of the Unwieldly \[Power\] \[Destroy\] \[Alteration\]
Tier 1.
Major Action. 2 Weave.
Melee range, 1 enemy
Attack: WIL+Rank vs Magic Defense
Effect: Success gives the target a -2 penalty to Physical and Magic defense for Rank rounds. This penalty cannot exceed the defense bonus the target gets from equipment.

> Additional Successes: increase duration by 2 rounds; increase penalties by 1
> Enhance Weave: 1 additional target (2); the penalty works on natural defense as well, but not defense gained from attributes (2)
