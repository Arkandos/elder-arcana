# Ripple Reform \[Power\] \[Transmute\] \[Alteration\]
Tier 2.
Minor Action. 2 Weave.
(5 + INT)m long 45° Cone, all enemies
Effect: Make a WIL+Rank Test against the Magic Defense of all targets. Success means they are Immobilized for 1 round. They may Rally STR (5+Rank) as a Minor action to end the effect.
The targeted ground area permanently becomes a Zone of Difficult Terrain.
> Enhance Weave: Increase the cone length by 2m (2); increase cone angle by 45° (2)
