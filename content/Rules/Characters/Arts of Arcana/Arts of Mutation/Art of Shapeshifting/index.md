Transform creatures in a temporary fashion. Limbs created through shapeshifting cannot wield weapons or shields. Botches may result in an unintended target, an unwanted form, or the inability to perform an action for a time.

| Rank | Sample effects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| :--- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Make cosmetic changes to a willing creature, like changing their skin or hair colour for a long time.<br>Shift your senses to any part of your body.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 2    | Transform a limb to a tool, or change the length of a limb.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 3    | Lengthen a limb to Close range while you focus. <br>Moderately enhance your agility, might, or tenacity like being able to lift heavier things or move through webs with ease.<br>Grow an additional appendage incapable of fine manipulation.<br>Become able to swim or climb at full speed for a moderate time.<br>Transform into a land animal of your size for a moderate time. |
| 4    | Enhance your senses unnaturally, like working in the dark or underwater while you focus.<br>Change your appearance while you focus.<br>Breathe water for a long time.<br>Create an autonomous appendage that follows your commands while in medium range.                                                                                                                           |
| 5    | Transform into a land animal of up to Large size, or a small animal of any other kind.<br>Turn a creature in Medium range mute or blind.<br>Increase or decrease your Size by one step while you focus.                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 6    | Grow a large set of appendages capable of fine manipulation.<br>Perfectly mimic the voice or appeareance of another creature while you focus.<br>Camouflage a creature to let it blend into its surroundings.<br>Gain an unnatural quality for a short time, like becoming spongy or boneless.                                                                                      |
| 7    | Create an autonomous appendage that you share senses with, that follows your commands while in Far range.<br>Gain a fly speed while you focus.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 8    | Increase or decrease your Size by two steps for a short time.<br>Gain a very unnatural quality for a moderate time, like no longer falling or not needing to breathe.                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 9    | Constantly shift your body, rapidly transforming between different shapes without additional time spent (spending Weave and Health instead)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
