# Lockjaw \[Power\] \[Transmute\] \[Shapeshifting\]
Minor Action. 1 Weave.
Effect: Make a STA+Rank (6) Test. Success means you gain the following Weapon for 5+Rank rounds.

Lockjaw \[Weapon\] \[Natural\]
Melee. Brawl damage. A target that takes damage is immobilized until it Rallies STR (5+Rank), either of you leave Melee range, or you attack using this weapon again.
