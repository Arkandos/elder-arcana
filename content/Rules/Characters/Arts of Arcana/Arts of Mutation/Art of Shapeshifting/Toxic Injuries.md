# Toxic Injuries \[Power\] \[Transmute\] \[Shapeshifting\]  
Minor Action. 1 Weave.
Melee, 1 enemy that suffered Physical damage this round
Effect: The enemy becomes poisoned, taking 1d6 Raw damage every round for Rank rounds. They may Rally STA (5 + Rank) to end the poison.
> Enhance Weave: Rank additional targets (2); increase the Raw damage by Rank (2);
