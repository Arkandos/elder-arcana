# Bearskin \[Power\] \[Transmute\] \[Shapeshifting\]
Major Action. 1 Weave.
Effect: Make a STA+Rank Test (6). Success gives you a +2 bonus to Physical Armor for 10 rounds (1 minute). While the effect lasts, your maximum Health is reduced by the Armor bonus.
> Additional Successes: Increase the Armor bonus by 2; increase duration by Rank rounds

> Enhance Weave: Increase duration by 1 Phase (2); at the end of the duration, if you took any damage, heal damage equal to the Armor bonus (1)
