# Backbarbs \[Power\] \[Transmute\] \[Shapeshifting\]
Major Action. 1 Weave.
Effect: Make a STA+Rank (6) Test. Success increases your rear Physical and Magic Armor by 2, and causes damage to attackers. When a creature attacks you from behind, they take 1d6+STA+Rank Physical damage. These effects last for 10 rounds (1 minute).
> Additional Successes: Increase the damage by 2; increase the duration by Rank rounds.

> Enhance Weave: increase Armor by 1 (2); increase duration by 1 Phase (2)
