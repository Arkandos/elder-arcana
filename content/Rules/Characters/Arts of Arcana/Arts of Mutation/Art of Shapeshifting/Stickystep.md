# Stickystep \[Power\] \[Transmute\] \[Shapeshifting\]
Tier 1.
Free Action. 1 Weave.
Trigger: You land on a solid surface
Effect: Make a DEX+Rank Test against a TN set by the GM, increased by how hard the surface is to cling to. Success means you stay attached to the surface (even if vertical) until the end of your next turn.
Every time you use this Power in a row, increase the TN by 5.
>Additional Successes: You may stay attached an additional round.
