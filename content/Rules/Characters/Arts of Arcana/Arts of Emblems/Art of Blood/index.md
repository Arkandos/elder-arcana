The emblematic powers of blood and sacrifice. Sacrificing blood means it is destroyed to fuel the effect. While all weavers can draw upon the power of their own blood by expending Health, those who wield the Art of Blood can do so with great efficacy. However wielders of Blood are watched with wary eyes (see [[The Weave#The power of Blood|The Power of Blood]]). Botches may result in terrible injuries, poisoning yourself, or causing harm to unwitting targets.
