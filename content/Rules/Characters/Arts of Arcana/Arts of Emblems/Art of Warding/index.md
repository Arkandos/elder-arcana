The emblematic powers of wards and antithesis. Warding protects you against something or someone, keeping them out. A ward placed on a location does not function if moved. Wards on locations can be broken, but not by a warded creature (even indirectly). Botches may cause you to ward off your self, attract instead of repulse, or cause you to believe the ward works.
