# Ward against Projectiles \[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Ward\] \[Force\]
Major Action. 3 Weave.
Melee, (3 + INT)m radius Zone
Effect: Make an INT+[[Weaving]] (8) Test. Success creates a Zone that weakens incoming projectiles for (5+Rank) rounds. Ranged attacks into the circle suffer a -4 penalty, and have their damage reduced by (Rank * 2).
>Additional Successes:  Increase the duration by Rank rounds; increase the damage penalty by 2.

>Enhance Weave: Increase radius by 2m (2); increase duration by 1 minute (1)
