# Line in the Sand \[Power\] \[Warding\] \[Force\]
Major Action. 3 Weave.
A (5+INT)m long line starting within Melee range.
Effect: Make a WIL+Rank Test (8). Success makes the line into a Zone of shimmering glyphs that prevent movement for 10 rounds. A creature attempting to pass through the Zone must succeed at a STR+[[Acrobatics]] Test of (8+Rank) to succeed, losing their movement if failed. If you create a new Line in the Sand, all previous ones end.

The Zone can be destroyed, by dealing (10 + Rank * 2) damage in a single hit against its defenses of (8 + Rank).

> Additional Successes: Increase the line by 1m; increase the Acrobatics TN by 2.

> Enhance Weave: Increase the line length by 1m (2)

Focus (Major, 3 Weave): You enhance the Zone, increasing its duration to 1 Phase and treating all variables as if your Rank was 3 higher. You may only Focus once on an individual Zone.
