Ask higher powers for guidance, induce visions and dreams. The answers are often vague or duplicitous and should not be taken at face value. Dream effects are active the next time you sleep, and require at least 1 hour of sleep. Botches might result in your senses being overwhelmed, drawing unwanted attention, or becoming unconscious.

| Rank | Sample effects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| :--- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Detect if a creature in Melee range is mostly virtuous or sinful. <br>Learn a common secret of someone in Close range, like a nickname.                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 2    | Ask a simple question, receive an answer of weal, woe, or unknown.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 3    | Have a cryptic vision of what happens if you perform an action in the immediate future.<br>When you dream, have a perfect recollection of the last day.                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 4    | Allow one sense to detect if a creature is mostly virtuous or sinful for a moderate time.<br>Detect if a group of creatures wishes to harm you while you focus.<br>View your own location from above or below.<br>Induce a dream into a specific creature within far range while they sleep.<br>Learn a poorly kept secret from someone in far range. |
| 5    | Send a message to someone familiar on the same plane the next time they sleep.<br>View your own location from high above.<br>Ask an outer entity a question and receive a vague answer.                                                                                                                                                               |
| 6    | When you dream, experience a different version of an event from a previous day, like what is behind a door you did not open (if you were able to).<br>Share the dream of another creature in far range.                                                                                                                                               |
| 7    | Have a short conversation with an outer entity, receiving cryptic answers.<br>Have a vision of an item you know of, seeing its surroundings.<br>Have a vision of the last owner of an item.                                                                                                                                                           |
| 8    | View a distant location you have previously seen, or a fuzzy vision of a location you have heard described.<br>Learn the best way to reach a location you have heard of.<br>Experience a waking dream, but suffer fatigue in return.                                                                                                                  |
| 9    | Gain the full attention of an outer entity, and receive a direct answer.<br>Induce a specific dream into an entire neighbourhood.<br>Permanently put a creature to sleep in Melee range.                                                                                                                                                              |
