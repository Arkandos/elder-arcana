# Rebounding Agony \[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Create\] \[Memory\]
Tier 1.
Major Action. 3 Weave.
Close range, 1 enemy
Effect: Make an INT+[[Weaving]] Test against the Magic Defense of the target.
Success means it takes half as much Raw damage as the largest amount of damage it took since your last turn (max 4 times your Rank). This power has no effect on constructs, elementals, or mindless creatures.
> Additional Successes: Increase damage by 2 (can go beyond the max allowed)
