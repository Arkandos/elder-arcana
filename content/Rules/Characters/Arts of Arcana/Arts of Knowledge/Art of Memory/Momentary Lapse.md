# Momentary Lapse\[Power\] \[Spell\] \[Destroy\] \[Memory\]
Tier 1.
Minor Action. 1 Weave.
10/20m, 1 enemy
Effect: Make an INT+[[Weaving]] Test against the Magic Defense of the target.
Success means they take a -2 penalty to Initiative for 1 round.
>Additional Successes: Increase duration by 2 rounds.
>Enhance Weave: Increase penalty by 2 (2); increase duration by Rank rounds (2)
