Manipulate your own and others memories. When modifying other’s memories, they may detect egregious changes as false. Botches usually result in a faulty memory, targeting the wrong creature, or receiving faulty information.

| Rank | Sample effects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| :--- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Perfectly memorise a small detail like a sentence for a day.<br>Detect if you have heard of something before, and where.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 2    | Share an image you remember with a willing creature in Melee range.<br>Add or remove a short memory in your own mind.                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 3    | Add a short memory to a creature while you focus.<br>Detect if a creature in Melee range has heard a name before.                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 4    | Share one of your memories with a willing creature in Melee range.<br>Make a minor change to a recent memory in Close range.<br>Perfectly memorise a large amount of information like a book, for many days.<br>Visit your mind palace, where recollection is much easier.                    |
| 5    | Add or erase a short memory from a creature within close range.<br>Add a very long memory to a creature while you focus.<br>Add the contents of a book to your mind palace permanently, which can be recalled whenever you visit it using this art.<br>Detect if a creature remembers a name. |
| 6    | Visit your mind palace together with a willing creature.<br>Share a memory with a group of willing creatures, or allow another to do the same.                                                                                                                                                |
| 7    | Rewrite all memories of a creature in Melee range, or turn them into a blank slate.<br>Rewrite a long memory of a group in Close range.                                                                                                                                                       |
| 8    | Perfectly memorise a very large amount of information, like the contents of a bookshelf.<br>Enter the memory of a creature in Melee range, experiencing it.                                                                                                                                   |
| 9    | Add or remove a short memory to all within a neighbourhood.<br>Detect any and all inconsistencies of a memory.<br>Expand your mind palace, opening a brief physical entrance into it.                                                                                                         |
