# Archer's Blessing \[Power\] \[Enhance\] \[Farsight\]
Major Action. 2 Weave.
Effect: Make a WIL+Rank (8) Test. Success means you gain a +1 bonus to Ranged Weapon attacks for 1 Phase.
> Additional Successes: Increase duration by 1 Phase.

> Enhance Weave: Increase duration by 1 Phase (2); increase the ranges of your Ranged Weapons by (2 * Rank) meters (1).
