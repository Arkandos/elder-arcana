# Moonlight View \[Power\] \[Transmute\] \[Farsight\]
Minor Action. 1 Weave.
Effect: Make a WIL+Rank Test (6). Success means you get low-light vision for Rank minutes. It doubles the distance you can see in light.
> Additional Successes: Increase duration by 2 minutes.

> Enhance Weave: Increase duration by 2 minutes (1); target Rank additional creatures in Melee range (2)
