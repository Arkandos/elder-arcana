Sense things beyond your normal senses. When using senses at a distance, you need to see the targeted point. Botches may result in your senses being overwhelmed, drawing unwanted attention, or receiving faulty information.

| Rank | Sample effects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| :--- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Shift your vision to somewhere in Melee range while you focus.<br>Sense the current time of day.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 2    | Predict natural events in the near future, like weather or floods.<br>Shift one sense to somewhere in Close range.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 3    | Enhance a sense so it works at a longer distance while you focus.<br>Share the senses of a willing creature while it remains in Medium range.                                                                                                                                                 |
| 4    | Shift all senses to somewhere in Close range while you focus.<br>Enhance a sense like seeing in the dark.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 5    | Allow one sense to detect spirits or magic while you focus.<br>Shift all senses to somewhere in Medium range you can see, or one sense to somewhere in Close range you cannot.<br>Create a visible mobile sensor that can move within medium range that you can use a sense through.          |
| 6    | Allow a sense to detect eavesdropping or divination magic.<br>Sense the direction of a known location, even if on a different plane.<br>Sense the location of portals and gateways, or recent teleportation within Far range.<br>Shift all senses to somewhere in Close range you cannot see. |
| 7    | Share the senses of a group of creatures while they remain in Far range.<br>Share the senses of a creature as long as you focus.<br>Shift a sense to somewhere in sight range, or to a familiar place on the same plane.                                                                      |
| 8    | Shift all senses to somewhere you are aware of while you focus, or perceive two locations at once.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 9    | Allow one sense to pierce a type of barrier, like fog, foliage, earth, stone.                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
