Abilities  
Abilities are how you handle most of your interactions with the world, as Tests are comprised of 1d10 \+ an attribute \+ an ability Rank. Abilities have two numerical values, Rank and Tier.  
Rank determines how invested you are in an ability, while Tier sets restrictions on when you have access to it. At the start of the game, you can only access Base Abilities and Tier 1 Adept Abilities.

# Ability Advancement

During the game, you can unlock new Abilities and improve the Rank of your existing ones by spending experience. Once you have Rank 1 of an Adept Ability, you may use it. You may only spend Experience and [[Rules/Characters/Arts of Arcana/index|Art points]] during [[Downtime]], but you can still undertake another activity at the same time.

| To purchase rank | Experience cost to raise from previous rank | Total Experience cost from rank 0 |
| :--------------- | :------------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------- |
| 1                | 5                                           | 5                                 |
| 2                | 10                                          | 15                                |
| 3                | 15                                          | 30                                |
| 4                | 20                                          | 50                                |
| 5                | 25                                          | 75                                |
| 6                | 30                                          | 105                               |
You should take care when spending your Experience, striking a balance between spreading out and stacking high. If you spend your Experience in only a few Abilities, your character becomes narrow and may lack the options to tackle a variety of challenges. By contrast if you spread out among too many Abilities, you delay your [[Echelons|Echelon]] advancement which puts you behind the curve.
