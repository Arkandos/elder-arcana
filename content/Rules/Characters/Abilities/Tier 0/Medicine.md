Treating injuries and ailments.
* *You must have 1 Rank to use this Base Ability*

---
Unlike other base abilities, Medicine could use some structure since its mechanical effects can be hard to contextualise compared to the magical abilities available at Tier 1 and higher.

When treating damage, add your Medicine Rank to the next Recovery the patient spends.
When giving long term treatment over an entire day, a successful Medicine Test allows the patient to ignore the Wound penalty on their Recoveries.
In general you can treat a number of patients at once equal to your Rank, but cannot treat yourself.
