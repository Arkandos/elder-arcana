# Spirit Speech \[Sense\] \[Blood\]
Tier 1. Prerequisite: [[Lore]] (Any but History) 1
Major Action. 1 Health.  
Effect: You can communicate with spirits, including with those who cannot normally communicate. To do so, make a PRE+Rank Test against their Mental Defense. Success means you can communicate for Rank minutes.

If you do not possess the relevant Lore ability for the spirit, you must achieve an additional success.

This Ability may be reversed, to instead stop a spirit from communicating. Make a PRE+Rank Test against their Mental Defense. Success means they cannot communicate as long as you Focus (Major) every round, and for Rank rounds afterwards.
If you are not focusing on this effect, they may Rally WIL (5 + Rank) to end it.
