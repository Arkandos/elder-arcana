# Quickening Strikes \[Transmute\] \[Body\] \[Blood\]
Tier 1. Prerequisite: [[Acrobatics]] 1
Free action. 2 Health.
Effect: Add this Rank to your Initiative before making the Test. When you make your first Melee attack this round, compare your initiative result with your targets. If you are 10 above or more, you may make a free Melee Strike against the target when their turn ends.
You cannot use this Ability if you cannot move.
