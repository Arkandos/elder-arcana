# Attune Item \[Create\] \[Esoteric\]
Tier 1
3 Phases
Effect: You attempt to Attune to an Enchanted Item, allowing you to increase its Attuned Rank. Make a WIL+Rank Test against the Attune TN of the item. If you are in a safe location (like a town) you can automatically succeed at a TN up to 10+Rank. Once attuned, you can spend experience to increase its Rank.
You can attune to up to Rank different items at once.

This Ability may be reversed to un-attune an item from its previous owner if they are still alive. If they re-attune to the item they keep their previous attuned rank.
