# Enchanting Music \[Enhance\] \[Illusion\]
Tier 1. Prerequisite: [[Artistry]] 1
Major Action. 
Effect: You use music to inspire emotions in up to (Rank * 20) people.
Make a PRE+[[Artistry]] Test against your audience's Mental Defense, with most non-weavers having a Mental Defense of 6.
Success inspires an emotion in them, its intensity climbing with your result. It lasts as long as you Focus, and for Rank hours afterwards.

These are merely sample effects, confer with your GM what may be appropriate TN for your effect.

|     Effect     | Target number |
| :------------: | :-----------: |
| Calm the upset |       9       |
|  Inspire love  |      12       |
| Incite a riot  |      15       |

Focus (Major) you affect an equal number of additional people (but no individual more than once).

This Ability may be reversed to attempt to block out all other sound than yours. Make the Test above, success means the affected people hear only you while you Focus (Major).
