# Favorable Impression \[Enhance\] \[Illusion\]
Tier 1. Prerequisite: [[Charm]] 1
1 phase.
Effect: Make a PRE+[[Charm]] Test against the Mental Defense of another character. Success improves their Attitude by 1 step, or 2 steps if you score 2 additional successes.
Their attitude reverts back to its original level of one step per day, or Rank days (your choice).
You cannot use this ability on the same target again until the effects have faded.
