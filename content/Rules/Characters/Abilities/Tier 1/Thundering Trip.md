# Thundering Trip \[Create\] \[Air\] \[Blood\]
Tier 1.
Free action. 1 Health.
Trigger: You hit with a Melee Strike.
Effect: The target must make a Knockdown Test if their Wound Severity does not increase. Add this Rank to the TN for the Knockdown Test.

You may also use this ability when you cause an enemy to make a Knockdown Test in Melee range to add (Rank / 2) to the TN for the Knockdown Test.
