# Weave Whispers \[Sense\] \[Esoteric\]
Tier 1. Prerequisite: [[Study]] 1
1 Phase or 1 downtime.
Effect: You can use this ability in two ways, to read magical writing and to identify enchanted items.

Read magical writing: Takes 1 Phase. Make an INT+Rank Test to understand magical writing. Each success grants you further insight into it. Failing by 5 or more may trigger hidden traps in the writing.

Identify enchanted item: Takes 1 downtime. At the end of the downtime, make an INT+Rank Test. Each success grants you information about the item and how to attune to it.
