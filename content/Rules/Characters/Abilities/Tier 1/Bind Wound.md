# Bind Wound \[Create\] \[Body\]
Tier 1\. Prerequisite: [[Medicine]] 1  
1 Phase. 5 Weave.
Effect: You attempt to treat [[Core Rules#Wounds|Wounds]] on yourself or a nearby ally. Make a DEX+[[Medicine]]+Rank Test and compare it to the table below. If you succeed to treat the Wounds, you cannot use this ability on them again until they have completed a rest, or their Wound severity increases.

| Wound severity | Result |                                                        Effect                                                         |
| :------------: | :----: | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------: |
|  Light Wounds  |   12   |                          The target ignores the Light Wound penalty until they have rested.                           |
|                |   15   |                                                 The Wound is removed                                                  |
| Medium Wounds  |   15   |                      The target treats the Wound penalty as 1 step lower until they have rested.                      |
|                |   18   | If you Bind Wound on the target again after their next rest and get a 15+, their Wound Severity is reduced by 1 step. |
|  Heavy Wounds  |   18   |                         The target treats the Wound penalty as 1 step lower until they rest.                          |
|                |   21   | If you Bind Wound on the target again after their next rest and get a 18+, their Wound Severity is reduced by 1 step  |
