# Beast Companion \[Enhance\] \[Nature\]
Tier 1.
Major action.
Close, 1 Beast
Effect: Make a CHA+Rank Test against the target's Mental Defense. Success means you improve their [[Core Rules#Relations|Relation]] to Neutral. Each day, you may repeat this Test to improve their Relation by an additional step, requiring 1 additional success than the step before. Friendly Beasts can even follow you into combat, while those of lower Relation prefer to stay out of it. In general only one Beast will follow you into combat at a time.

This ability is not any form of mind control, and the beast will only treat you as well as you treat it.

---
This Ability may be reversed to instead turn a Beast against its former master. Make the same Test as above, but Success reduces their relation to their Master to Neutral instead. If you score two additional Successes the relation is reduced to Hostile instead.
