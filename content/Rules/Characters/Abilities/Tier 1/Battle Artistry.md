# Battle Artistry \[Create\] \[Illusion\] \[Blood\]
Tier 1. Prerequisite: [[Artistry]] 1
Major Action. (up to Rank times / round). 1 Health.
Effect: Make a PRE+[[Artistry]] (6) Test. For every success, you and all allies within (Rank * 10)m gain a +1 bonus to all Melee attacks and Evade Tests for Rank rounds.

This Ability may be reversed, instead affecting all enemies within the same range. Make a PRE+[[Artistry]] Test against their Mental Defense. Each success gives them a -1 penalty to all Tests for Rank rounds.
