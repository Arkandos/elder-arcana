# Steel Mind \[Control\] \[Mind\] \[Blood\] \[Evade\]
Tier 1.
Free action (up to Rank times / round). 1 Health.
Trigger: An effect would succeed against your Mental Defense.
Effect: Make a WIL+Rank Test against the triggering Test. Success means you are unaffected by the triggering effect.
Evade: You can only use one Evade ability against a particular effect. Evade Tests only suffer Wound penalties (not other Test penalties)
