# Dispel Magic \[Destroy\] \[Esoteric\]
Tier 1. Prerequisite: [[Weaving]] 1
Major Action. 2 Weave.  
Effect: Choose a magical effect within Close range. Make a WIL+Rank Test against (6 + the target effect Rank). Success ends the effect. An effect created by a ritual or attached to an item cannot be ended, but is instead suspended for (Rank * 2) rounds.
