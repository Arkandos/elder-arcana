# Mounted Stability \[Enhance\] \[Body\]
Tier 1.
Effect: Add this Rank to your, and your mount's Knockdown Tests while you are mounted. If you fail a Knockdown Test by 5 or less, you do not fall off your mount.
While Mounted, you can [[Kip Up]] using Ride in place of Acrobatics.
