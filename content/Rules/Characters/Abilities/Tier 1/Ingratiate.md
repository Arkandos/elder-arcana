# Ingratiate \[Enhance\] \[Illusion\]
Tier 1. Prerequisite: [[Charm]] 1
Major Action.
Effect: Make a PRE+[[Charm]] Test against the Mental Defense of another character. For every success you gain a +2 bonus to social tests against them for Rank hours (max bonus equal to twice this Rank). 
You can only use this Ability once per day per target.
