# Taunt \[Control\] \[Mind\] \[Blood\]
Tier 1. Prerequisite: [[Command]] 1
Minor Action. 1 Health.
Effect: Make a PRE+[[Command]] Test vs the Mental Defense of a creature you can see. For each success they take a -1 penalty to Mental Defense, and to all Tests that are not against you for Rank rounds.

This Ability may be reversed as a Major Action, to instead affect all creatures within Medium Range. Make the Test as above. In addition to the normal effects, the targets gain a +1 bonus to all Tests against you for the duration.
