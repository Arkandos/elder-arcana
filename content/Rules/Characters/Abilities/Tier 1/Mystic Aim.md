# Hawk Aim \[Sense\] \[Force\] \[Blood\]
Tier 1.
Minor Action. 1 Health.  
Effect: If you have not moved this turn, make an INT+Rank Test against a target's Magic Defense. Each success gives you a +2 bonus to Ranged Strikes against them until the end of your turn.
You cannot move more this turn.
