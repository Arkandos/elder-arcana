# Demoralize \[Enhance\] \[Illusion\] \[Blood\]
Tier 1. Prerequisite: [[Command]] 1.
Minor Action. 1 Health.
Effect: Choose a creature within Close range and make a Pre+[[Command]] Test against their Mental Defense. For every success they suffer a -2 penalty to all Tests until the end of the next round. Every time you use this against the same target in an encounter, you take a -3 penalty.
