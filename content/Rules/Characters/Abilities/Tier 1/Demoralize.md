# Demoralize \[Enhance\] \[Illusion\] \[Blood\]
Tier 1. Prerequisite: [[Command]] 1.
Minor Action. 1 Health.
Effect: Choose a creature within Close range and make a Pre+Rank Test against their Mental Defense. For every success they suffer a -2 penalty to all Tests until the end of the next round. Every time you use this ability against a target in an encounter, you take a cumulative -3 penalty to further uses against that target.
