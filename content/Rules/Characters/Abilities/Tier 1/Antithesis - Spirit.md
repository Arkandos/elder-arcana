# Antithesis - Elemental \[Ward\] \[Force\]
Tier 1. Prerequisite: [[Lore]] (Any but history) 1
Major Action. 3 Weave.  
Effect: Choose a spirit within Medium range. Make a WIL+Rank Test against their Magic Defense. Success means they are immobilized and unable to act other than communicate as long as you Focus (Major) on this effect.
The target may Rally WIL (5 + Rank) to end the effect.

This Ability may be reversed to instead create a (Rank) meter radius ward on the ground within Melee range. Choose a lore type. Whenever such a spirit would enter the ward or attack into it, make a WIL+Lore (Magic) Test against their Magic Defense. Success means the attempt is negated, while failure means they become immune to the ward.

For both effects if you do not possess the appropriate Lore ability for that spirit, you require an additional success.
