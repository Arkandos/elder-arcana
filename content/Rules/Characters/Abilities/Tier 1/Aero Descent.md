# Aero Descent \[Control\] \[Air\] \[Blood\]
Tier 1. Prerequisite: [[Acrobatics]] 1
Reaction. 1 Health.  
Trigger: You fall
Effect: Make a DEX+Rank (6) Test. Success means you do not take damage for falling up to (Rank * 100)m.
If you score 2 additional successes, you may control your descent and land anywhere within a number of meters equal to the distance fallen.

This Ability may be reversed to cause you to fall faster. Each time you double the speed you fall, increase the TN of the Test above to not take damage by 2.
