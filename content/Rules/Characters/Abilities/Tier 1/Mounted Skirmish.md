# Mounted Skirmish \[Create\] \[Force\]
Tier 1. Prerequisite: [[Ride]] 1
Effect: When your mount moves at least half its Speed you may add this Rank to the damage of your next Melee attack. Enemies attacked by you after your mount has moved in this way cannot take [[Opportunity Attack]]s against you or your mount.
