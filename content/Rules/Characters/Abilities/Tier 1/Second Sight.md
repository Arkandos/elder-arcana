# Second Sight \[Sense\] \[Illusion\]
Tier 1\.  
Major Action. 1 Health.
Effect: Make an INT+Rank Test. For Rank minutes, you can see incorporeal spirits whose Magic Defense you succeed against.. Those that are formless appear to you in a way that your mind could understand them. For example, a disembodied faerie stag could appear as a vague 4-legged animal.

Alternatively, you may attempt to detect invisible things by comparing your result against a target's Magic defense. Success means you see the outline of the invisible thing for Rank rounds.
