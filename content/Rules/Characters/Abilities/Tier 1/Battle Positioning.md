# Battle Positioning \[Control\] \[Body\] 
Tier 1.
Free action (1 / action)
Trigger: You make a Strike.  
Effect: Before or after resolving the Strike, choose 1 unlocked effect below (+1 effect for every 2 Ranks). Every Rank, choose a new effect to unlock.
* Advance (Melee): You push the target 1m and shift 1m towards it. Targets larger than you move half as far, while smaller targets move twice as far.
* Lure in (Melee): Shift 1m away from the target. It may move 1m towards you. The target must be able to move to use this option.
* Gambit (Melee): Slide the target 1m (which cannot leave your reach), and then move to keep your relative position to them.
* Double tap: The target must either shift 1m laterally or you gain a +2 bonus on your Strike.
* Step: Move 1m
