# Outmaneuver \[Enhance\] \[Body\] \[Blood\]
Tier 1. Prerequisite: [[Acrobatics]] 1
Minor Action. 1 Health.  
Effect: Make a DEX+Rank Test against the Physical Defense of a creature in Melee.
Each success gives you a +2 bonus to Physical Defense against them, and an equal bonus to your next Melee Attack against them until the end of the round.
