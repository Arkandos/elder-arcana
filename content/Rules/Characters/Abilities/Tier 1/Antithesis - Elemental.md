# Antithesis - Elemental \[Ward\] \[Force\]
Tier 1. Prerequisite: [[Lore|Lore (Magic)]] 1
Major Action. 3 Weave.  
Effect: Choose an elemental within Medium range. Make a WIL+[[Lore|Lore (Magic)]] Test against their Magic Defense. Success means they are immobilized and unable to act other than communicate as long as you Focus (Major) on this effect.
The target may Rally WIL (5 + Rank) to end the effect.

This Ability may be reversed to instead create a (Rank) meter radius ward on the ground within Melee range. Choose an elemental type. Whenever such an elemental would enter the ward or attack into it, make a WIL+Lore (Magic) Test against their Magic Defense. Success means the attempt is negated, while failure means they become immune to the ward.
