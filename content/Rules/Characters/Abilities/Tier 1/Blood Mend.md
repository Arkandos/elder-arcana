# Blood Mend \[Create\] \[Blood\]
Tier 1.
1 Phase. 2 Health.
Effect: You create an elaborate ritual and draw your own blood. Decide how many additional Recoveries you wish to gain.
Then make a STA+Rank Test, opposed by a test made by the GM. The GM rolls 1d10, +3 for every Recovery you wish to gain. If your test exceeds the GM you gain the desired Recoveries until you take a rest. Failure means you take raw damage equal to the difference between your test results, and you increase your wound severity. If you were already at max severity, you are unconscious for a day.

You may only use this ability once per day.

This Ability may be reversed, to instead spend the blood of another living creature in Melee range. Decide and Test as normal, but regardless if you succeed or fail the creature takes raw damage equal to the GM's test result. Being known to have reversed this ability causes most kith to treat you as hostile.
