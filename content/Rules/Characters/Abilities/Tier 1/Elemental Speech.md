# Elemental Speech \[Sense\] \[Blood\]
Tier 1. Prerequisite: [[Lore|Lore (Magic)]] 1
Major Action. 1 Health.
Effect: You can speak the elemental languages of air, earth, fire, and water. For every Rank, you learn one of the languages.

To communicate with an elemental you must know their language, and make a PRE+[[Lore|Lore (Magic)]] Test against their Mental Defense. Success means you may communicate with them for Rank minutes. Additional successes grant you insight into their behaviours.

This Ability may be reversed, to transform all languages of a willing target into an elemental language for Rank minutes. You must still make the above test for every elemental you wish the target to communicate with.
