# Flighty Shot \[Enhance\] \[Air\] \[Blood\]
Tier 1.
Free action (up to Rank times / round). 1 Health.  
Trigger: You make a Ranged Strike
Effect: Extend both ranges of the Weapon by (Rank * 5)m.

This Ability may be reversed, to instead reduce your ranges by the same amount to negate weather penalties. Make a DEX+Rank Test against a TN of the weather, success means you take no penalty to the triggering Strike.
