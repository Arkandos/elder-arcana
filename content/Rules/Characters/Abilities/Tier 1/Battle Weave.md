# Battle Weave \[Create\] \[Esoteric\] \[Blood\]
Tier 1.
Free action (up to Rank times / round).  
Trigger: You increase a target's wound severity with a melee attack, or defeat them
Effect: You regain 1 Weave, or (Rank / 2) Weave if they were defeated (min 1)
