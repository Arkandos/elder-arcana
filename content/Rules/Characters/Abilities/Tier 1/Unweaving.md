# Unweaving \[Destroy\] \[Esoteric\]
Tier 1. Prerequisite: [[Weaving]] 1
Major Action. 2 Weave.
When you gain this talent, choose either Weave Aura or Weave Warp.
* Weave Aura: You gain an Aura (Rank * 4)m which lasts for Rank rounds. When you use this talent, and the start of your turn while it lasts make a WIL+Rank Test. Compare the result to the Magic Defense of all creatures in the Aura (and any who later enter). They take a -2 penalty to Weaving Tests until they leave the Aura for each Success scored.
* Weave Warp: Choose a (Rank * 4)m radius within Medium range. You create a Zone there for Rank rounds. Then make a WIL+Rank Test. Compare the result to the Magic Defense of all creatures in the Zone (and any who later enter). They take a -2 penalty to Weaving Tests while in the area for each Success scored.

When a Weaving Test is made into the Aura or Zone, compare your Test result with their Magic Defense and reduce the Weaving Test as above.
