# Unweaving \[Destroy\] \[Esoteric\] \[Evade\]
Tier 1. Prerequisite: [[Weaving]] 1
Major Action. 2 Weave.
When you gain this talent, choose either Weave Aura or Weave Warp.
* Weave Aura: You gain an Aura (Rank * 4)m which lasts for Rank rounds. When you use this talent, and the start of your turn when it lasts make a WIL+Rank Test. Compare the result to the Magic Defense of all creatures in the Aura (and any who later enter). They take a -2 penalty to Weaving Tests until they leave the Aura.
  The same effect applies to Weaving Tests into the Aura.
* Weave Aura: Choose a (Rank * 4)m radius within Medium range. You create a Zone there for Rank rounds. Then make a WIL+Rank Test. Compare the result to the Magic Defense of all creatures in the Zone (and any who later enter). They take a -2 penalty to Weaving Tests while in the area.
  The same effect applies to Weaving Tests into the Zone.
