# Shield Block \[Enhance\] \[Alteration\] \[Blood\] \[Evade\]
Tier 1
Free action (up to Rank times / round). 1 Health.
Trigger: An attack hits your frontal defense.
Effect: Reduce the damage by Rank. Then you take only half the damage of the attack, and your shield takes the other half (possibly destroying it).
Evade: You can only use one Evade ability against a particular effect.
