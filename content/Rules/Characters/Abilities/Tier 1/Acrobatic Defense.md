Tier 1\. Prerequisite: [[Acrobatics]] 1  
Minor Action. 1 Health.  
Effect: Make a DEX+Rank against the highest Physical Defense of all enemies engaged in Melee with you. You gain a \+2 bonus per success to Physical Defense against everyone you succeed against until the end of the round
