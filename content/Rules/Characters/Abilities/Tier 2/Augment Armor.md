# Augment Armor \[Enhance\] \[Alteration\]
Tier 2. Prerequisite: [[Craft]] 3
Downtime
Effect: You attempt to augment an armor. Make a STR+Rank (8) Test. Each success increases either its Physical or Magic Armor by 1. The maximum total augment you can achieve is limited by your Rank.

The maximum bonus a wearer can benefit from is equal to their Echelon.
