# Force Net \[Create\] \[Force\] \[Blood\] \[Vigilance\]
Tier 2.
Free action. 1 Health.  
Trigger: You make an Opportunity Attack
Effect: Make a DEX+Rank Test against the target's Magic Defense. Success means they cannot move until the end of the round.

Vigilance: For every 3 Ranks of Vigilance Abilities you have, you can make one Opportunity Attack per round without spending a Reaction.
