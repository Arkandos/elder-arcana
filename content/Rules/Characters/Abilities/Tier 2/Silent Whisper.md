# Silent Whisper \[Create\] \[Illusion\] \[Blood\]
Tier 2\.  
Free Action. 1 Health.  
You attempt to speak directly to up to Rank targets within (Rank \* 10)m. Make a PRE+Rank Test against the targets Magic Defense (or 6 if the target is willing). 
Success means the target hears you as if you were right next to them regardless of other noise in the area.
