# Poisoned Words \[Control\] \[Mind\] \[Blood\]
Tier 2. Prerequisite: [[Guile]] 3
Major Action. 1 Health.
Effect: Make a PRE+Rank Test against the Mental Defense of a target you are speaking with. Success means you direct the target's anger towards someone else. This could deflect blame from something you've done, or create animosity towards the chosen scapegoat. The effect lasts for Rank minutes, +1 minute per additional success. When the duration lapses, the target remembers what actually happened.

You may affect more than one creature you are speaking with, by targeting the highest Mental Defense of the group and suffering a -1 penalty for each additional creature.

This ability is ineffective against creatures that are engaged in combat with you, and you require two additional successes to affect them.
