# Uncanny Pursuit \[Enhance\] \[Body\] \[Blood\] \[Vigilance\]
Tier 2.
Free action. 1 Health
Trigger: After you make an Opportunity Attack.  
Effect: You may move up to half your Speed towards the target of the triggering attack.

Free action. 1 Health
Trigger: An enemy makes an Opportunity Attack against you.
Effect: Add this Rank to the targeted defense, or an Evade Test against the attack. If this bonus makes the attack miss you, you still take half damage from that attack (but no other effect from it).

Vigilance: For every 3 Ranks of Vigilance Abilities you have, you can make one Opportunity Attack per round without spending a Reaction.
