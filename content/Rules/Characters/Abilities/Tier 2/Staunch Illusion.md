# Staunch Illusion \[Enhance\] \[Illusion\] \[Blood\]
Tier 2.
Reaction. 1 Health.  
Trigger: A creature would Disbelieve one of your illusions.  
Effect: Add this Rank to the TN to Disbelieve temporarily. This may cause the triggering creature to fail to see through it.

This Ability may be reversed as a free action to reduce the TN by the same amount to see through the illusion permanently.
