# Blood for Blood \[Control\] \[Blood\]
Tier 2. Prerequisite: [[Medicine]] 3
Major Action. 
Effect: Choose a willing or helpless creature in Melee range and make a WIL+Rank Test. The result is how much damage you may transfer between yourself and the target. This does not change wound severity, but either target might become unconscious or die.

Using this Ability to transfer damage to a helpless creature is considered a repulsive act, and a character known to have done so is met with hostility by other kith.
