# Mask Art \[Destroy\] \[Illusion\] \[Blood\] 
Tier 2.
Major action. 2 Health.  
Effect: You attempt to hide the fact that you are using an art. Make a PRE+Rank Test with a penalty equal to the Tier of the Art effect you wish to pruduce. The result of the test is the TN to detect the use.

This Ability may be reversed to mask the use of one art as another you possess. Make a PRE+Rank Test, the result being the TN to detect you using the original art.
