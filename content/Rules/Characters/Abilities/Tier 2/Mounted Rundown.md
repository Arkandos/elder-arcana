# Mounted Rundown \[Enhance\] \[Force\] \[Blood\]
Tier 2.
Minor action. 2 Health.  
Effect: This turn your mount may pass through the spaces of enemies that are smaller than it, and enemies you pass through in this way cannot take Reactions against you. The maximum number of enemies you may pass through is this Rank.

When you or your mount hit with a Melee Strike this turn, the enemy must make a Knockdown Test regardless of the damage taken, adding half this Rank to the TN.
