# Nature Speech \[Sense\] \[Blood\]
Tier 1. Prerequisite: [[Lore|Lore (Natural)]] 3
Major Action. 1 Health.
Effect: You can speak with animals and plant life.

To communicate you must make a PRE+[[Lore|Lore (Natural)]] Test against the targets Mental Defense. Success means you may communicate with them for Rank minutes. Additional successes grant you insight into their behaviours.

---
[[Symbiont|Symbionts]] with this Ability reduce the Health cost by 1.
