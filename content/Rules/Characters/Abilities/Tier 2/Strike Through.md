# Strike Through \[Create\] \[Force\] \[Blood\]
Tier 2.
Free action (1 / round).
Effect: Once per round, you can spend an additional Success on a Strike to attack a secondary target in range. Resolve the first Strike as normal. Then make a Strike against the secondary target, using DEX+Rank to attack.
