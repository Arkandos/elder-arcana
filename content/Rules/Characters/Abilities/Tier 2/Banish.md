# Banish \[Destroy\] \[Convoking\]
Tier 2.
Major action. 1 Health.  
Effect: Make a PRE+Rank Test against the Magic defense of a spirit or summoned creature within Medium range. Success means they are banished.

If you attempt to banish a spirit connected to an art you do not possess at Rank 3 or higher requires an additional success.
If you attempt to banish a creature you have not summoned, you require an additional success.
