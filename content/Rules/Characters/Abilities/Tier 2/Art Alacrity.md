# Art Alacrity \[Enhance\] \[Esoteric\]
Tier 2.
Major action. X Weave. 
Effect: You attempt to use an Art very quickly, which is a risky proposition. You must always make a roll to use an Art this way, and pay X Weave, where X is twice the Rank of the effect being produced.

You can only produce effects with an Art Rank that is (This Rank - 3) or lower (minimum 1).
