# Art Alacrity \[Enhance\] \[Esoteric\]
Tier 2.
Major action. X Weave. 
Effect: You attempt to use an Art very quickly, which is a risky proposition. You must always make a roll to use an Art this way, and pay Weave equal to the Rank of the effect being produced.

You can only produce effects with an Art Rank that is (This Rank - 2) or lower (minimum 1).
