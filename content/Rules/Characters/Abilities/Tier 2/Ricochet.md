# Ricochet \[Control\] \[Force\] \[Blood\]
Tier 2. Prerequisite: [[Weapons (Ranged)]] 3
Minor action. 1 Health.  
Effect: Your next Ranged Strike this turn may ricochet up to Rank times. Make a DEX+Rank Test vs a target's Magic Defense. Success means the Strike may ricochet off terrain and obstacles to hit them.
