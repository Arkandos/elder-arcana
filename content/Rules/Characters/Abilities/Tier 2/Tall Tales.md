# Tall Tales \[Enhance\] \[Mind\] \[Blood\]
Tier 2. Prerequisite: [[Guile]] 3
Effect: Add this Rank to any [[Guile]] Test to lie. While this Ability is very good at concealing the truth, the tall tales it creates can be hard to accept. It's much easier to convince a guard on the street that you are just moving along (and not lurking in the shadows) than it is to convince someone that you should be given the keys to the royal vault.

This Ability may be reversed, to instead make a truth you speak more believable, adding this Rank to any Test to convince someone of the truth.
