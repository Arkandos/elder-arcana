# Common mounts
Mounts that are not combat trained flee in dangerous situations unless controlled by [[Ride]] Tests.
Riding gear is not included in this price, and costs around 1/3rd of the mount price. Without proper riding gear, the Ride ability is required to ride them at all, and even then at a penalty.
Prices are listed in silver, unless otherwise noted.
