1. Choose a [[Rules/Characters/Kith/index|Kith]] and note down its features.  
2. Choose your starting [[Characters#Attributes|Attributes]]. Attribute bonuses from Kith are applied after your choices.  
3. Assign your starting Base Abilities.  
   4. Assign the numbers from the following array as the Rank of any [[Rules/Characters/Abilities/Tier 0/index|Base Abilities]] you wish: 3, 3, 2, 2, 1, 1. Do note that no ability or art may be above Rank 3 in character creation!
5. Spend 25 Experience on any Abilities of Tier 1, or Base Abilities.  
6. Spend 20 Art points on any [[Rules/Characters/Arts of Arcana/index|Arts]], and pick 3 powers from among those Arts.   
7. Spend 150 silver on starting [[Rules/Equipment/index|Equipment]].
