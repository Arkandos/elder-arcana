Talents are a way for a character to further specialise in their Abilities and Powers. Talents modify the Ability or Power they are associated with (from here referred to as the Talents source), and their effects are cumulative. This means that multiple Talents can modify the same source as long as their effects are not contradictory.
# Acquiring Talents
To acquire a Talent, you must fulfil its prerequisites. One of these is always possessing the source. Once you fulfil the prerequisites, you may during [[Downtime]] spend Experience to acquire the Talent (just like you would when acquiring an Ability) equal to its Experience cost.
# Using Talents
Whenever you use a Talent's source, you may choose to apply any number of applicable Talents you possess. You can in this way pick and choose when you wish to apply a benefit, and when you would rather use the original source.
