# Protecting Shield Block \[Talent\]
Tier 2. Prerequisite: [[Shield Block]]. Experience Cost: 10
Effect: When an ally in your reach is attacked, you may use Shield Block on them. When using this Talent, any damage you would take is instead suffered by the affected ally.
