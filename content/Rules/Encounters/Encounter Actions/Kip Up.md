**Kip Up \[Basic\]**  
Minor Action. 1 Health  
Effect: Make a DEX+[[Acrobatics]] - Initiative penalty (6) Test. Success means you stand up from Prone. This Test ignores the penalty from being [[Rules/Encounters/Conditions & Modifiers/index#Prone|Prone]].
