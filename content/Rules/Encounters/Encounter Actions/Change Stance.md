**Change Stance**  
Free action when your turn starts.  
Effect: Choose one effect that lasts until the start of your next turn:
* Offensive: You gain a \+3 bonus to Melee attacks. You take a \-3 penalty to your Physical and Magic Defense.  
* Defensive: You get a \+3 bonus to Physical and Magic Defense. You take a \-3 penalty to all Tests that are not defensive. You gain a +3 bonus to Defensive Tests like [[Knockdown|Knockdown Tests]], [[Kip Up]], [[Rally]], and \[Evade\] Tests.
