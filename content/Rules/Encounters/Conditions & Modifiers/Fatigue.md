When you overexert yourself, you risk becoming fatigued.  For every point of Fatigue, you take a -1 penalty to all Tests. To reduce it by 1 point takes 2 hours of rest.

---
For every 2 ours beyond 8 spent travelling you must make a STA (8) Test. Failure means you gain a point of Fatigue.

Usually it takes 10 hours of sleeping if taking watches to rest, or 8 hours otherwise. For every 2 hours not sleeping, make the Test above. If you get less than 4 hours of Sleep it means you regain no Recoveries or Mythos.
