When you would gain Weave while suffering from Weave Burn, reduce the amount gained by your Weave Burn. Then reduce your Weave Burn by the amount of Weave you did not gain.

For example, if you have Weave Burn 3 and would regain 2 Weave then you regain 0 Weave and reduce your Weave Burn by 2 (to 1).
