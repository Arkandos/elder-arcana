# Neutral pantheon

These gods do not interfere much in the goings on of the mortal world. Indeed, they seem to not care much at all.

## Lyzaris - Goddess of Death
Cares for the cycle of death, life and rebirth. Does not involve itself in the petty squabbles of the other gods, except to keep the Cerulean pact. The only god that does not reside in the Divine realms. Lyzaris is highly revered, but has few worshippers. There is no central organisation like a church of Lyzaris, instead each congregation has its own structure.
## Dhozotz - God of the End Times  
Represents time, decay, but not death. The end of all things and the inevitable march of time. Believed by some to be the source of daemons. Worshippers of Dhozotz are usually shunned or labelled as insane.

# Light pantheon

These gods are generally seen in a positive light by the people of Coret, drawing their power from the worshipping masses. Worshippers and temples to the gods can be found in many places. Most people pray to multiple gods depending on their current needs. Despite being the “light” gods, they all have good and negative traits. And those that displease the gods may face their wrath.

## Thaeus - God of War
Violence, war and natural disasters.

## Ylene - Goddess of Growth 
Fertility (but not love), plant growth, merchants, abundance. Partner of Catelia.

## Catelia - Goddess of Passion
Love, resentment, arts, grief. Partner of Ylene.

## Theelia - Goddess of Water
Water, moon, disease, healing. General fluids.  
Appears as an older woman in simple but brightly coloured clothing. Her hair appears to be made of gently flowing water.  
One of the oldest of Corets deities, having arisen in the early stages of creation before land or sky. A common religious symbol are two rivers that meet and form a droplet.  
Theelia is kind and gentle to those she cares about, but brings her wrath to bear against those who harm her protectorates.
## Sajun - God of Light 
Forgiveness, tranquility.
## Phaedromus - God of Order
Civilization, construction, crafting, law, memory. Nemesis of Qiarin. Husband of Vatinia.
## Chalter - God of Travel 
Travel, music, impulse, doorways.  
Once a mortal human minstrel and storyteller. Legend goes that he travelled around the wide world, seeing the wonders and terrors of Coret. At some point, he must have travelled beyond the confines of the material and into the astral plane.   
Chalter is impulsive and lackadaisical, believing that one's heart's desires can be found at the end of the road, or over the next hill. The journey is what matters, not the destination. His acts are not random however, he aids those whose journeys would be enhanced by it.
## Vatinia - Goddess of Nature
Nature, children, wisdom, hunting, animals. Wife of Phaedromus.

## Dark Pantheon

These gods are viewed negatively, oftentimes worse than the infernal lords themselves. For the dark pantheon are ill-willed and unpredictable, wishing to steer the world in dangerous directions. Their worshippers hide their faith, and congregations are often small in fear of being discovered. They are hunted, exiled and imprisoned. Perhaps for good reasons.

## Qiarin - God of Secrets 
Dreams, hatred, secrets, mysteries, plots. Nemesis of Phaedromus.  
Qiarin is a meddler with a desire to know all. Not to spread this knowledge and improve the world like Phaedromus, but to keep it hidden from everyone else.

## Oion - Goddess of Weather
Weather, misfortune, famine, heat.  
Oion is one of the most cursed names on Coret (along with the Overlords), being blamed for all manner of mishaps. Regardless of it being true or not. Oion revels in the attention regardless.

## Vizarin - God of Mysticism
Arcane magic, fate, divination, curses, insanity  
While associated with arcane magic, Vizarin is not the source of it. Although he would very much like people to think so. Vizarin knows much about the intricacies and potentials of magic, which leads many powerhungry mages to seek his aid.
