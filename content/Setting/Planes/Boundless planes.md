The boundless planes are the Astral, the Graveyard, and the Void.
## Astral plane

The astral plane is shapeless and infinite, stretching out like an endless ocean between the planes like a connective tissue. It can be traversed in several ways, which depends on how you entered it. The most common forms are a silvery ocean, or a winding path in a blank landscape.

## The Graveyard

The Graveyard is where the souls of kith go when they die. Here they are placed in the long waiting line to enter the black gate and be judged by Lyzaris. It is a strange and sometimes nonsensical realm as described by those that have returned from the dead. Looking towards the gate, you see the endless sea of people around you waiting their turn, and the petitioners both divine and infernal fighting over the souls. Turn around, and your discorporated essence appears to be in a desolate landscape stretching out to the horizon.  
Once a soul has been processed by Lyzaris, it is sent to its final resting place, from where only the most powerful of rituals can retrieve them. A virtuous and devout soul goes to one of the divine realms, while a corrupted and sinful soul goes to the infernal to have their sins ‘harvested’ before being discarded as an empty husk. A small minority remain in the graveyard in a state of limbo, aiding in the processes of the Graveyard.

## The Void

While all the boundless planes are infinite, the Void is an empty abyss. Nearly nothing exists here, no soil, sun or water. All that stumbles into the Void is eventually destroyed by the Daemons, who harbour such hatred against all of existence that they want nothing more than to see it all unravelled. Daemons claim to be as old as existence, in constant torment from the pain of existing.
