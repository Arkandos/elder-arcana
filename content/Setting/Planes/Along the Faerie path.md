Faerie is the polar opposite of the [[Mysteries of the Magic planes|Magic planes]].  It is ever changing, never constant, and *wants* you to come. Faerie is the easiest plane to enter, of all the planes actively trying to draw kith close. Just like the other realms, Faerie is alive and has purpose.

[[Faerie Creatures]] are varied and constantly changing as they are reshaped to fit whatever tale they have taken a role in.
