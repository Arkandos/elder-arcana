# Elementals
Creatures that consists purely of one of the four elements are known as elementals. A pure living flame, an animate wind, or a rolling boulder are all examples of elementals. They are pure of form, consisting of a singular natural element. They have achieved sentience through a high level of magic concentration. Usually they spontaneously coalesce from natural material in areas with powerful magical auras, but they can also intentionally be created by a Weaver using appropriate [[The Weave#Essences|essence]]. The stronger the aura and the larger the availability of an elemental source the stronger the potential elemental. A large and deep lake with a powerful aura may create a massive water elemental, for instance.

Elementals are simple beings, that exist with with simple purpose. All elementals are mobile, can react to and perceive their surroundings despite having no conventional sensors. Each type of elemental senses their surroundings in different ways. Fire elementals sense differences in temperature and flammability. Water elementals are aware of moisture and liquid in their vicinity, earth elementals sense by vibrations in the ground and by the contact of earthen material. Air elementals are the most aware of all, having acute awareness of all things in contact with the air and understand sound.

Having simple minds, elementals seek to fulfil basic "needs". Air elementals have the simplest of desires, for all they wish to do is to move unimpeded. They loathe all barriers to their path, but are also naturally curious due to their enhanced senses. Fire elementals have the most primal of desires, for they are constantly fading. They seek fuel to burn and cold to turn to heat. Earth elementals have an unquenchable thirst and seek moisture, especially that which can be drained from living things. Water elementals are curious creatures, that seek to investigate and experiment with all substances and liquids they can access. Unfortunately for kith, this means they often drown them.

Their simple minds and composition do make them easy to ward against and control by those who know the proper techniques. Their aptitude for hiding and blending into natural environments can make them effective guardians or scouts, as long as the controller has a way to communicate.

# Airy Spirits
Airy spirits or sometimes called unseen spirits are invisible magical spirits that exist in all parts of the world. They are most prevalent in magical auras, where the air swarms with them (to those that can see such spirits). Contrary to most other types of spirits, they have little to no interest in the material world. Thus they are the unseen spirits, whose presence is not noticed by the vast majority of individuals. They may exist inside all things, both living and natural (but generally not tied to creatures). In a magic forest, every tree could have its own spirit.

The most common airy spirits are those connected to natural things, like rocks, plants, or water. But in magical auras spirits may coalesce from more abstract concepts like emotions, or a location.
