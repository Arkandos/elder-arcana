The Magic plane is the oldest of the planes of existence, the first to exist after [[Boundless planes#The Void|The Void]]. It is sometimes referred to as the Elder plane, or the uncaring plane due to the fact that it or its denizens have no interest in the acts of the material world. It is divided into a number of sub-planes, one for each of the 4 elements and one for the plane of creation. It is incredibly unusual for anyone to enter any of the magic planes. Indeed, no one has ever entered the plane of creation and returned to tell the tale. Not even the [[Gods Above|Gods]] have ever visited, they can only retell what information they can sense about it.

# The Elemental Planes
There is a sub-plane for each of the four natural elements, consisting almost exclusively of their related element. Thus the plane of fire is a raging inferno filled with fire and magma, while the plane of water is a massive ocean of ice and water. Few Kith ever enter one of the elemental plane, both due to the dangers involved but also due to the lack of rewards for such a dangerous journey. The planes are largely desolate, consisting mostly of their respective elements. However there is a significant concentration of essence, but the extraction of it proves too much for most individuals.

# The Plane of Creation
The plane of creation, the perfect plane. The most persistent enigma of existence has many names. For nobody has ever entered it and returned to tell the tale. Even the act of entering it requires great power and knowledge, for it is like digging into bedrock with bare hands.
However, what can be gleamed by indirect experimentation, knowledge, and divination is that the plane of creation holds a perfect copy of all things. These are not pale imitations like those of the [[Along the Faerie path#Shadow Realm|Shadow Realm]], but the perfect ideal of all things in existence. When an object is created, or a healing weave is woven one draws upon the power of creation. Does this mean that a copy of all things exist in the plane of creation, or does one appear when first interacted with in such a way? There is no way of knowing, for when one investigations such an idea do you create the object you observe?

When magic repairs an object or creature, it attempts to restore it to the state of it in the plane of creation.

# Wheel & Weave
While the Weave permeates all realms, nowhere is it stronger than in the Magic planes. Most of the magical auras in the material plane are a result of where the magic plane intersects the material and bursts through. This combined with the fact that the magic realm is the oldest of the planes has led many scholars and philosophers to propose the theory that the Weave originates in the Magic plane and spreads from there to the rest of existence. However there has been no way to confirm this theory, and there are many alternate ideas. One of which is that the Weave is so strong in the magic planes due to how pure and empty they are, that in that way there would be more "room" for the Weave.
