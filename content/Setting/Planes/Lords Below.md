Each of the 7 infernal realms is controlled by a duke or duchess reigning supreme as the epitome of that sin. Infernals meddle in affairs all over Coret, trying to corrupt the population and fill them with sin. 

## Am-heh - Duchess of Pride
Appears like a humanoid “egyptian” woman clad in the most exquisite and extravagant clothing and jewelry. Often portrayed tossing away one goblet for another.  
Am-heh is the original of her title, no recorded names of any predecessor. Her arrogance and suspicion has allowed her to subvert any would-be upstarts with claim to her title. However these aggressive purges of her underlings have made her realm functionally weaker than than any but Sloth.  
She absolutely believes she is the foremost of all the Infernal Lords and has no reason to improve or compete with the others, for how can one improve upon perfection?

| Sinful traits of Pride | Opposite traits |
| :---- | :---- |
| Arrogant, haughty, rebellious, suspicion, vanity | Loyalty, dedication, faithful, humble, reliable, trusting |

## Awargh - Duke of Sloth 
Appears like a slumped over humanoid with extended bones and faces covered in stretched skin. Awargh's real head hangs low, between his legs. Many fleshy tentacles stretch out, slowly writhing.  
He is one in the long line of lords of Sloth. The oppressive realm imposes rapid decay on all its inhabitants, and in this way the current lord is regularly replaced. Awargh failed upwards in the hierarchy, as others were too cynical or shy to take the position themselves. Unlike the other infernal lords, Awargh is far from the most powerful in his realm. They simply do not care to improve.

| Sinful traits of Sloth | Opposite traits |
| :---- | :---- |
| Cowardly, cynical, indecisive, lazy, shy | Bold, courageous, determined, ready, zealous |

## Iztix - Duchess of Greed
Iztix is a most terrifying sight to behold, few that have seen them have ever been able to give a description, with most denying the encounter happening at all. Interesting enough, two descriptions have been; an enormous beast with countless legs and arms and a horned eyeless head. The other is an eerie humanoid female where one side is withering away.  
Due to the aforementioned effects, not much is known about Iztix other than their penchant for disease and never-ending desire for new sensations. The realm of Greed is a varied one, which attracts many types of selfish and sickly infernals. It's uncertain if they intentionally spread diseases to the material plane, or if they just accidentally end up there.

| Sinful traits of Greed | Opposite traits |
| :---- | :---- |
| Gluttonous, needy, selfindulgent, sickly, weak | Healthy, pious, steady, temperate, vigilant |

## Maleagant - Duke of Envy
A shifting aberration of eyes, ears, and mouths.  
Maleagant absorbed the previous two dukes of envy, obsessed with the idea of obtaining the powers of all the other infernal lords. To eventually envelop all of the known realms combined, Maleagant is by far the largest threat to the infernal status quo. They are exceptionally cruel even for a duke and tailor each punishment to its recipient to inflict maximum torment. Maleagant’s agents of disruption meddle in the affairs of all the realms, sometimes even its own.

| Sinful traits of Envy | Opposite traits |
| :---- | :---- |
| Cruel, gossip, gruff, jealous, meddlesome | Kind, charitable, compassionate, forgiving, generous, merciful |

## Onomoth - Duke of Avarice
An imposing humanoid figure with a rotating set of nine horned heads with glowing red eyes. Often depicted with six arms, each wielding a different implement of power.  
Onomoth, Taltaroth, Sastreth, or Kath’matal. The Duke of Avarice is known by countless names. Among the infernal lords he is often considered the most dangerous, as his plots and manipulations reach far through both Coret and the other planes. Many scholars believe that not even the Duke himself can keep track of all the schemes he is involved in. For all the grand plans and machinations of the Duke, they are just as likely to fail as to succeed. For the realm of Avarice is ripe with corruption, and their own personal schemes counteracting each other.

| Sinful traits of Avarice | Opposite traits |
| :---- | :---- |
| Ambitious, corrupt, cunning, devious, manipulative | Even-handed, fair, honest, honorable, straightforward |

## Rirzi & Tiraz - Duke and Duchess of Wrath
Uniquely, the realm of Wrath is currently divided between the children of the previous lord. Both Rirzi and Tiraz lay claim to the throne of Wrath after having murdered their father Meloc together.  
Rirzi has a female humanoid form with long claws, curled hornes and a long billowing dress made of magma and flesh. A blazing fire bursts from the center of her head, with eyes like hot coals.  
Tiraz has a beastly form, twisted melting face and long straight horns running on all fours with melted feet. Two long black wings protrude from his back, the only majestic feature he retains.  
After they had just delivered the killing blow to their father, they started fighting each other. An equal fight, Tiraz retreated before they would have destroyed each other. Now constant clashes rage across the Realm of Wrath, as both siblings attempt to overthrow the other.

| Sinful traits of Wrath | Opposite traits |
| :---- | :---- |
| Angry, desperate, spiteful, vengeful, violent | Calm, cheerful, hopeful, optimistic, patient, peaceful |

## Valytha - Duchess of Lust
Valytha is to many the perfect mate. Having the ability to appear as the most pleasant and arousing creature that the onlooker can imagine, regardless of that may be. Gender may be hard to define among infernals, but Valytha identifies herself as a woman regardless of how she may look.  
Second of her title, Valytha has ruled over her realm for hundreds of years without opposition. Without exception, all inhabitants of the realm are her fanatical followers in both heart and soul. None harbour any ill will toward her, not plot her demise. This gives her the strongest position within her realm of all the infernal lords. Her favored approach to both mortal and immortal foes is to snare them with a silvered tongue and masterful manipulations.

| Sinful traits of Lust | Opposite traits |
| :---- | :---- |
| Fanatical, lecherous, naive, overconfident, reckless | Careful, cautious, chaste, practical, prudent |
