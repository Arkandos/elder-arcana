Undeath is a general term applied to all those creatures that represent unlife after death. This includes the [[Spirits Incorporeal#Ghosts|ghosts]] that kith leave behind, the spirits that pass to the [[Boundless planes#The Graveyard|Graveyard]], as well as all those raised as unliving material creatures.

Undead are not inherently malevolent, but the majority are hostile to the living for one reason or another. Ghosts wish to resolve their worldly ties beyond all else, the spirits of the Graveyard wish to be left alone. The raised unliving are often controlled by external forces, or have had their minds twisted into something that despises the living.
# The Unliving
For a body to rise from the dead, a magical power must act upon it. This could be a necromancer raising skeletons to serve as mindless minions, a curse taking hold, or the corrupting influence of a magical aura.

An undead creature with no spirit is mindless. It only acts like the magic that created them commands. If that magic fails, they are returned to nothing more than an inanimate body. It is no more or less evil than any other construction.

An undead creature with a spirit has an intelligence, even if it may be limited. A controlled undead must act as its master commands (but may creatively interpret the instructions to their own benefit), while an unbound undead acts as they want.

# Kith and the Undead
Across Coret, undead creatures exist in all shapes and sizes. All Kith encounter the undead at some point, like a stray ghost or the minions of a local necromancer. 

The average reaction to them varies a lot on the region. Many places react with fear or disdain for the undead, seeking to hunt them down. The mere sight of them may cause a panicked stampede, or the call to arms. Some places like the Ebon Empire treat undead (at least the intelligent ones) like full citizens, and they are a common sight in any major settlement. 

[[Spirits Incorporeal#Ghosts|Ghosts]], while undead, are more respected. They are seen as unfortunate souls prevented from reaching a peaceful afterlife. Some cultures hold a great deal of respect for their ancestors, which they summon and commune with to ask for guidance.
