Beyond [[Coret]], lies the mystical planes and realms. Their denizens all have vested interests in Coret, and affect the world in various ways. The planar categories are Magic, Divine, Infernal, Faerie, and Boundless.
# Mystical Auras
Each plane exude their own powerful auras that affect everything in their domain. Within a plane or realm, that aura is absolute and at its most powerful.

When a plane leaks through the material world, it creates a mystical aura aligned to that plane (and realm, if possible). If multiple auras overlap, the more powerful one is dominant and the others are submissive yet still active.

Auras from the different realms function and appear in different ways.
# The Magic plane
**[[The Elder Planes|The Magic plane]]** is the most uncaring of them all and perhaps the oldest. There is no unified agenda or desire to influence Coret, and most interactions occur since the Magic plane spills over into the material world. It is divided into the elemental planes (air, earth, fire, water) where the raw elements dominate, but also the plane of Creation, where a perfect version of all things exists. Healing and repairing magic draw upon Creation, trying to restore the target to its perfect state in that plane. The Magic planes are inhospitable, and few have ever returned from their journeys there (if those tall tales can even be believed).

# Divine Realms
**The Divine** is where the self proclaimed [[Gods Above|gods]] reside, both good and bad. Through their worshippers and divine agents they try to influence the world and shape it in their image, and to gather more devotees. However, they can no longer directly touch the world. The Cerulean pact binds them all to noninterference, although many gods are stretching the limits more and more these days. As the number of worshippers wane, so do the power of most gods. Desperate to reestablish their dominance they're pushing the limits of what is acceptable.

# Infernal Realms
**The Infernal** houses the 7 realms of the [[Lords Below|dukes and duchesses of sin]]. The ruler of each realm is the pinnacle of the appropriate sin. As a whole the Infernal seek to corrupt the Kith of Coret filling them to the brim with sinful energy. Once the corrupted pass to the Graveyard, Infernal petitioners bargain for their soul. If they succeed, the soul is dragged to the Infernal where the sin is squeezed out of them. The realms divided, they squabble mostly internally. If they could ever be united, they would be a force capable of overwhelming even the Divine.

# Faerie Realms
**[[Along the Faerie path|The Faerie]]** plane is the one most contingent on Kith, even more than the Divine. While the power of the divine is related to their worship, Faerie cannot exist without the belief of Kith. [[Faerie Creatures]] live and breathe only due to the fact that Kith believe in them. Not even their bodies are fully real, consisting of solid illusions named glamours. Faeries all exist to extract more of this imagination from Kith, either consciously or unconsciously. They do this through enacting stories that will live on in the minds of Kith, continuously siphoning power from it.

# Boundless planes
**The** [[Boundless planes]] are the Graveyard, the Astral, and the Void. They are more nebulous than the other planes.  
The Graveyard is where the souls of all Kith go when they die. The god of death, [[Gods Above#Lyzaris - Goddess of Death|Lyzaris]], rules over this realm and is the only god existing outside the Divine. Divine and Infernal petitioners arrive here, fighting for custody over the souls.  
The Astral is the connective tissue between realms. It has been described as an endless silvery ocean, or a winding road in a blank landscape. Those who know how to astral travel can use it to travel great distances.  
The Void is where the daemons reside. The daemons claim to be as old as existence, and they *hate* existing. They wish for nothing more but the complete unravelling of the universe.


## Magic Auras
Magic auras are the most prevalent on Coret, occurring spontaneously in natural constellations. Due to their stable nature, magic auras are often ancient and attract magical creatures.

Inside a magic aura, the world seems more vibrant and alive than outside it. The air is clear, the sounds sharp, and colours striking. The presence of the Weave is strong, and any weaver can draw upon this fount of power to fuel their Arts. This makes magic auras a popular place for conducting rituals and arcane experiments.

## Divine Auras
Each divine god has their own aura, competing with the aura of all other gods. Divine auras appear due to the actions of their devout followers, and are often spread out over larger areas like cities. Particular locations like a temple may be dominated by a single god's aura. Over time, a divine aura fades if not refreshed by the devout.

All cities exist within an amalgamation of divine auras, where generally no single one takes the upper hand. But for those places where a particular divine aura is dominant, the connection to that god is more powerful, and the connection to all others is dampened. Aspects of the deity are more prevalent, and the influence of the other planes is dampened.

Despite not being divine, [[The Overlord's empire]] seems to have a smothering effect on all faithful except the [[Gods Above#Lyzaris - Goddess of Death|Lyzarites]].

## Infernal Auras
Each infernal realm spews forth its aura towards Coret with their corrupting influences. Infernal auras are created through intense or repeated acts of sin. A battlefield might not have an aura of wrath, but the mass grave of an execution would. Sinful acts are those that are made with malice, or in excess.

Infernal auras have a tendency to spread, as their corrupting influence pushes more individuals to propagate the connected sin. All those who spend time in an infernal aura are corrupted by their influence, over time becoming twisted [[Infernal Creatures#Corrupted and Befouled|corrupted creatures]].

Weaves created in an infernal aura are tarnished by the corrupting influences, but boosted in strength. This enticing idea leads many weaver's astray and to fall into the clutches of the infernal, following a promise for more power.

## Faerie Auras
Faerie auras are entirely created and controlled by their resident faeries. Within it, they hold a great deal of power in influencing "reality" with the power of their [[Faerie Creatures#Glamour|Glamours]]. The purpose of a faerie place is to draw kith into their stories, so most often they are wondrous and attractive.

The nature of a faerie aura depends on the faerie realm it is connected to, and the story to be told. But experiences within are otherworldly and often surreal. Weaves made within are not directly affected, but may appear far more powerful than they truly are. It is not unusual that Kith seek out faerie places with strong auras, and are never heard from again. Perhaps the life inside is too enticing to leave, or they never made it out alive.

Faerie auras are the most unstable of all, directly relying on both the belief of Kith and the glamourous powers of its faerie residents. If either falters, they quickly collapse. If another aura dominates over a faerie aura, the faerie aura quickly subsides as the belief of the area shifts to the dominant aura.

## Boundless Auras
While the boundless planes are capable of generating auras on Coret, they are usually weak and fleeting. Scholars disagree whether this is due to the boundless planes not reaching out towards Coret like the other planes, or that Coret somehow resists their influence.

Graveyard auras are often cyclical, tied to constellations of the stars and lunar sphere. For a short time, burial places become graveyard auras where the dead and living can connect.

Astral auras exist only where portals to the astral plane exists. Nowhere else does its aura seem to seep into the world. Within such auras senses are befuddled. Caution should be taken, as the unwary traveller may slip into the astral plane and become lost forever.

Void auras are just like their parent plane a place of pure destruction and mayhem. Even the sturdiest of materials rapidly decay. The air is dry and thin, the ground withered, and the sky dark even on the clearest summer day.
