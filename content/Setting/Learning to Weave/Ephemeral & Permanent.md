When [[The Weave]] can create potentially any type of matter, how come resources are still valuable on [[Coret]]? The reason is the ephemeral nature of a woven effect. Over time a weave unravels itself until it completely fades. An earthweaver might create a block of pure steel, but it will inevitably disappear once the threads unravel themselves. Only a weave stabilized by utilising [[The Weave#Essences|essence]] is permanent. Such a creation stabilized by essence is no longer a magical creation, but real and indistinguishable from the ritual that created it (once the residues of the ritual have faded). A magical effect made permanent through essence remains magical, but will never unravel on its own. An example would be an illusory wall made with essence will never fade over time, but is still an inherently magical effect.

# Nourishment
Inevitably one comes to the topic of magically created nourishment. Does created bread fill you? Does a woven glass of water sate your thirst? The question is complicated, but one answer is clear.

Created food and drink cannot sustain you, as its temporary nature cannot be absorbed into your body as permanence. Such nourishment will not stave off hunger or hydrate your body. However created food and drink still tastes well and fills your stomach. But you derive no nourishment nor energy from them. In a way, they are a mirage or illusion. No known temporary weave has ever created functioning nourishment.

# Health of Body and Mind
As previously discussed, a weave cannot permanently change something. Once it unravels, it disappears. Thus a Body weave to heal injuries works only temporarily. Once the duration runs out, the wounds once closed reopen. A similar weave stabilized with essence means the body remains healed, but most do not consider spending essence on such endeavours work the risk and cost.

A more viable approach is to stimulate the natural healing process instead. Since the weave only accelerates the healing is not undone once the duration fades. But there are limits, the weave can only push the body so far and once the internal reserves have been expended it cannot be pushed beyond its limits.

# Permanent resources
So is magic useless when it comes to increasing your resources? Absolutely not! It simply requires a different mindset. Instead of outright creating the resource using valuable essence, you utilise magic to find, harvest, and refine the resources.

Imagine a weaver that requires vast amounts of wood. They could spend large amounts of essence to create it, but that would be very costly. Instead they could plant real trees. Greatly accelerate their growth and then magically cut them down to size. Since the magic is only performing temporary work, nothing is undone when the weave unravels. This process takes longer than using essence, but is far faster than a mundane method. Clever application of magic is the greatest asset in any weaver's toolbox.
