Arcane Connections are mystical connections between objects or beings. Arcane Connections exist between parts of a whole, or between meaningful attachments. The existence of Arcane Connections can be sensed by [[Weave Watcher]]s, or by any Weaver meditating upon an object. Due to the nature of these connections, their source cannot be confirmed by magical means. Only by using magic targeted through a connection and seeing what is affected can one learn where they lead.

While there may be any number of potential connections from one object, only the strongest one functions as its Arcane Connection.

# Parts of a Whole
Whenever a piece of something is removed, it remembers its source as an Arcane Connection. A branch is an Arcane Connection to its tree, even after they have been separated. A rock split into four parts means that each part is a weak connection to the others, because there is no longer a majority to tie them together. Likewise water from a river is a very weak connection to that river, since as soon as it is removed it starts forming connections with its new location. Water from a pool would have a stronger connection, due to staying in one location.

Parts of a beings body are connections to that being. Thus their blood, locks of their hair, or even severed body parts are all connections of varying strength. In general, blood is the strongest of these connections. The greater the quantity of something, the stronger the connection.

As parts are removed they lose their connections over time, something called [[#Arcane Decay]].

# Meaningful Attachments
Arcane Connections can also be formed by meaningful attachments to either a location or being. This could be due to a lengthy time in each others presence, or by strong emotional ties. A rock that has been in a cave since the dawn of time is a strong connection to that cave. A wedding ring worn for a many years would be a strong connection to that bearer. A lovingly crafted sword is connected to the smith that forged it.

In this way, most objects hold Arcane Connections. But due to the fleeting nature of the Weave, they fade over time through [[#Arcane Decay]].

# Arcane Decay
Just like any other magical effect that has not been made permanent, Arcane Connections unravel themselves over time. A branch removed from a tree is connected to that tree for a while, but slowly loses that connection as others become dominant. For example if crafted into a staff the new dominant connection would be to the crafter, or to the person using the staff. Water rushing through a river has little time to form connections with the water around itself or the location it is travelling through. This makes the connection *weak*.

When magic is used to affect something through its Arcane Connection, it can affect it regardless of distance to the target. 

The strength of a connection determines how long it remains dominant. A *strong* connection has a much higher relative strength than the next, which means it will remain dominant for long. A *weak* connection by contrast is easily overwritten by a new influence.

# Emblematic Connections
There is a second type of mystical connections called Emblematic Connections. These cannot occur naturally, but are instead created for a specific individual. On their own, emblematic connections have no use.  But use in concordance with Arcane Connections the power of the connection is amplified.

Emblematic connections are such things as knowing the target's birthname, or secret nickname. Knowing what day they were born, and where. A visual representation (like a painting) of the target is also an emblematic connection to them but must be made by the person making use of the emblem.
