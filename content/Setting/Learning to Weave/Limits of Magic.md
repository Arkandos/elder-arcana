While [[The Weave]] is ever present and growing in strength, it is not omnipotent. Ceretain acts are beyond even the most powerful weavers on [[Coret]]. These are commonly known as the limits of magic.
# The Limits of Magic
## Time
The weave cannot change the past or future, nor can it alter the flow of time. You cannot travel back or forward in time, but you can learn from the past as it is already set. And you can glimpse into one of countless possible futures.
## Stellar
The weave cannot alter the stellar cycles. A weaver could darken the skies and black out the sun, but never move or alter any stellar bodies.
## Sustaining
Temporarily created food or drink [[Ephemeral & Permanent#Nourishment|cannot sustain a living creature]].
## Ephemeral
Any weave not stabilized with [[The Weave#Essences|essence]] is temporary and will unravel itself over time.
## Soul
The weave cannot create nor destroy [[souls]], as they seemingly exist beyond the flow of the Weave. At most, a weave can trap a soul and force it to serve a purpose but this is considered and exceptionally cruel and evil act.
## Essential essence
[[The Weave#Essences|Essence]] can never be transformed nor created. Essence is pure concentrated weave, and beyond manipulation. Once essence has been spent, it can never be recovered.

# Subject to limits
What about the powerful otherworldly entities out in Coret and beyond? Can they surpass the limits? The answer seems to be no, they are just as bound as any mortal weaver. While [[Gods Above|gods]] may cause miracles of great power, they are nevertheless bound by the same limits. Many believe that they use massive amounts of essence without much effort to create the lasting effects that remain as reminders. The same is true for the [[Lords Below|infernal lords]], and all other supernatural creatures. The limits of magic are fundamental forces of the cosmos.

Some scholars debate the fact that [[Faerie Creatures]] and the [[Along the Faerie path|faerie realms]] seem to surpass these limits, especially within their own realm. But the most learned faerie scholars have refuted these theories, stating that as creatures of trickery and illusion faeries are simply very apt at appearing to surpass the limits with clever use of their powerful [[Faerie Creatures#Glamour|glamours]].
