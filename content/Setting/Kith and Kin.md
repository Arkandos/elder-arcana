The many sentient species of Coret are referred to as Kith, and all have the capability to become weavers. What follows is some brief information about the most common types of kith.
# Humans
**Humans** are one of the most numerous races of Coret. Despite their short lifespans, humans are a force to be reckoned with. They learn and adapt quicker than any other kith, and have a natural aptitude for riding. Humans can be found in almost any society, and their adaptability makes them get along with most other kith.
# Dwarves
**Dwarves** are long-lived, short, and sturdy. Nicknamed the children of the rock, most dwarven societies are underground. Stubbornness and a can-do attitude permeates dwarven culture, that with enough elbow grease and effort anything is possible. Dwarven craftsmen are renowned and a point of pride, and so are their merchants. Dwarves value the journey just as much if not more than the destination.  Elves and dwarves are natural friends, both enjoying the beauty of craftsmanship and bonding over long lives that gives them unique perspectives of the world. This doesn't mean that their views align, but their differing viewpoints makes for lengthy discussions and competitions of pride.
## Dwarf weavers
Dwarves value creation, legacy, and respect for those that came before. In dwarven culture, the arts of [[Rules/Characters/Arts of Arcana/Arts of Thaumaturgy/Art of Earth/index|Earth]], [[Rules/Characters/Arts of Arcana/Arts of Thaumaturgy/Art of Fire/index|Fire]], and [[Rules/Characters/Arts of Arcana/Arts of Mutation/Art of Alteration/index|Alteration]] are the pillars of society, central to construction and craft. Their love for tradition and desire for legacy favors the arts of [[Rules/Characters/Arts of Arcana/Arts of Calling/Art of Necromancy/index|Necromancy]] and [[Rules/Characters/Arts of Arcana/Arts of Knowledge/Art of Memory/index|Memory]]. 
The vagueries produced by [[Rules/Characters/Arts of Arcana/Arts of Knowledge/Art of Augury/index|Augury]] leaves much to be desired by most dwarven scholars, who prefer their tomes of history.
How an object or piece of art was created is every bit as important as the final result. This means illusions are seen as cheap tricks, and illusionists are looked down upon by most dwarven settlements.
# Elves
**Elves** are tall and long-lived, with eerily symmetrical faces. They are agile and perceptive, but their slim build leaves them vulnerable to the dangers of the world. Elves are more adaptable than most other kith, even if it may take them multiple generations to fully acclimate. Elves have made their homes in the depths of ancient forests, in magnificent underground cities, even at the bottom of the sea shielded by protective domes. No matter their origin, wild or highborn, elves feel a special connection with nature and thrive the most in its presence. Elves and dwarves are natural friends, both enjoying the beauty of craftsmanship and bonding over long lives that gives them unique perspectives of the world. This doesn't mean that their views align, but their differing viewpoints makes for lengthy discussions and competitions of pride.
## Elven weavers
Elves are closely attuned to nature and have a natural inclination for the [[Rules/Characters/Arts of Arcana/Arts of Thaumaturgy/index|Thaumaturgic]] arts, especially the arts of [[Rules/Characters/Arts of Arcana/Arts of Thaumaturgy/Art of Nature/index|Nature]] and [[Rules/Characters/Arts of Arcana/Arts of Thaumaturgy/Art of Water/index|Water]]. Elves esoteric nature and desire to improve the world means that the arts of [[Rules/Characters/Arts of Arcana/Arts of Knowledge/Art of Augury/index|Augury]] and [[Rules/Characters/Arts of Arcana/Arts of Manipulation/Art of Illusion/index|Illusion]] are much appreciated in elven societies.

# Giantbloods
**Giantbloods** or half-giants is a common term for all those who have heritage stemming from the larger folk. Larger, stronger and tougher than the average kith, giantbloods are well renowned warriors that take pride in honor and valor. While less perceptive than the average kith, they are far more quickwitted than the giants they hail from. Giantblood communities are usually nomadic tribes that spend a surprising amount of time trading with other tribes and communities. Giantbloods that enter other kith societies find themselves welcome, but that their size often causes problems. Giantbloods and sprites have a special kinship, sharing both humour and troubles of size.
# Sprites
**Sprites** are tiny winged humanoids about the size of a rabbit. They are quick, and have a special aptitude for influencing other kith. Sprites claim that it’s due to their cute size and positive demeanor, but anyone knowledgeable about sprites knows that the true reason is due to their faerie origins. Sprites are all glamour-clad, with some being true cognizant faeries and others just having strong faerie blood. Sprite societies are very uncommon, consisting of at most a couple of families. Instead, they usually prefer to spread themselves out into the communities of other kith, where their trickster nature is more or less welcome. Giantbloods and sprites have a special kinship, sharing both humour and troubles of size.

Sprites as player characters are mortal kith with strong faerie blood and not [[Faerie Creatures|true faeries]].
# Mythkin
**Mythkin** is a catchall term for those individuals who have magic in their blood since birth. They seem to have fate’s favor, being blessed with natural luck and resistance to hostile magic. Depending on the society, mythkin are praised as good luck charms or shunned as a magnet that draws luck away from others. Many mythkin have a natural aversion to others of their kin, meaning it takes a special connection for them to congregate.
# Shifters
**Shifter**s is what people call those shapechanger kith that lurk among them. Naturally distrusted, many societies pin blame for much illdoing on shifters. Some shifters take pride in their capabilities, while others hide in plain sight hoping to never be revealed. Shifters learn at an early age how to deceive others, and how to stay out of trouble. Whether this is the chicken or the egg in the stories about them is unknown.  
Shifter societies are rare (at least openly)
# Elementalkin
**Elementalkin** is the group term for those attuned to one of the four elements. Thus there are air, earth, fire, and waterkin. Some owe their heritage to elementally attuned creatures, others to some transformative event. While some elemental kin see their fate as a curse, most feel empowered. Elementalkin have a humanlike build with clear strokes of their associated element. They generally congregate in small nomadic societies or form clans in permanent settlements of other kith.
# Celestials
**Celestials** are what those with divine heritage are called, descendants of the gods and their kin. Just like the damned, they are visually as varied as the gods themselves. Celestials have the weakest of bloodlines, and several generations may lapse before the celestial traits re-emerge. Thus celestial groups are uncommon, and generally have little relation to each other. Other kith treat celestials either as a benevolent proof of the gods presence, or a painful reminder how mortals have been left behind and ignored by the divine.
# Damned
**Damned** is the negative word used for those with infernal heritage. While some have indeed been cursed, most are connected to infernals by blood. Just like celestials, they are visually a varied kin depending on their heritage. Damned often congregate together, finding comfort in shared discrimination. Other kith generally treat damned with suspicion, wondering what nefarious deeds they have been chosen to fulfill. While the damned have as much free will as any other kith, it seems to be a recurring pattern of them falling into behaviors that serve the realms of sin.

# Symbionts
**Symbionts** are plant-humanoid hybrids born from relations between mortal kith and powerful magic spirits of nature like dryads. Their bodies exist in a state of symbiosis between humanoid and plant intermingled into one being. Symbionts often have unusual skin with vibrant colours or the texture of bark. Some symbionts have strangely coloured hair while others instead have flowing plants where hair would be. 

They are ponderous creatures and drink in the sight of the world as if it was fresh spring water. Symbionts feel a strong kinship with others of their kind, despite having no relations. For they are sterile and cannot have offspring of their own (without the aid of magic). Their lone nature makes them social outcasts as most have spent the majority of their lives away from civilisation. Their plant nature affects their bodily functions, the most notable being that their blood is as thick as sap. They require less food than kith of their size, but much more water. If deprived of sunlight they will begin to wither and suffer as if starving, their plant nature wilting away.
